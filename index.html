2 — The Model (v4)

A lumped-parameter ODE thermal model with chemistry, fouling, vent, and operational realism. Every term traces to a specific finding in one of the six audits. Equations:

k(T) = A · exp(−Ea / R·T) Arrhenius rate g(X) = 1 + 50 · σ((X−0.20)/0.04) · σ((0.85−X)/0.04) Trommsdorff sigmoidal turn-on + vitrification cutoff inhib(I) = σ((I_crit − I)/0.005) binary-with-smoothing inhibition (not (1−I) damping) dX/dt = k · (1−X) · inhib(I) · g(X) dI/dt = −c_inh · k − 1e−3·I · H(X − 1e−5) MEHQ + O₂ cliff (triggers at X > 10 ppm) δ(X) = min(δ_max, f_plate · X · m / (ρ_PMMA · A_cool)) fouling layer thickness (CAPPED at 3 mm) U_eff(X) = 1 / (1/U_clean + δ(X)/k_PMMA) Q_evap = h_m · A_cool · (p_sat(T_film) − RH·p_sat(T_air)) · MW_H₂O · h_fg / (R·T_film) Q_crack(t) = Cd · A_crack · √(2·ρ_vapor·ΔP) · ΔH_vap_MMA for t ≥ 64 h (TIME-gated, not T-gated) Q_IR = ε·σ·A_ext · (T_shell⁴ − T_sky⁴) net radiative loss (sky ≈ 50°F at night) ops = coverage_frac · duty_cycle operational realism: 0.30–0.60 typical withdraw = H(T − 150°F) OCFA withdrawal trigger collapses cooling to 10% Q_cool = (U_eff · A · (T − T_water) + Q_evap + Q_crack) · ops · (1 − 0.9·withdraw) + Q_IR − Q_solar(t) dT/dt = (Q_gen − Q_cool) / (m · C_p), Q_gen = m · ΔH_p · dX/dt Survival constraints (importance-sampled weights, not hard rejects): HARD reject: effective threshold (BLEVE − stratification) crossed before hr 75 SOFT weight: Φ((peakF_by_hr_74 − 100 + σ_g) / σ_g) censored-Gaussian gauge likelihood

What's new in v4 vs prior versions:

  • Importance sampling. Hard "T ≥ 95 °F by hr 74" replaced with a soft censored-Gaussian likelihood. Acceptance jumped from 0.2% (v3) to 68% (v4).
  • I₀ prior collapsed. Was bimodal (50% fresh / 50% aged); fresh trajectories couldn't reproduce the observed 77→90 °F rise so were deterministic waste. Now aged-only.
  • Operational realism. Wetted-shell coverage ~0.5 × duty cycle ~0.9 multiplies the deluge cooling — portable monitors aren't a 100% film source.
  • Crack-vent time-gated. Was T-gated near boiling; per McGovern the crack is venting NOW at 100 °F. Bernoulli-vapor flow magnitude ~5–25 kW.
  • Net IR radiative loss. ~10 kW continuous nighttime cooling that prior versions missed.
  • Withdrawal trigger. If bulk T > 150 °F, OCFA pulls personnel; cooling collapses to ~10%. Captures real IC decision logic.
  • Hot-spot stratification. Gauge reads bulk T but the reaction zone is the hotter top layer. Effective threshold = sampled BLEVE T − sampled ΔT_strat (median ~20 °F).
  • Trommsdorff onset moved to X=0.20 with sigmoidal turn-on + vitrification cutoff at X=0.85.
  • Fouling self-limiting at 3 mm. Old monotonic formula gave 4 inches of PMMA at X=0.5 — physically absurd.
Calibration honesty. The model is a phenomenological lumped-parameter system, not a first-principles simulation. The pre-exponential A is calibrated to reproduce the observed ~1 °F/hr early rise. All other constants trace to published values (NIST, Polymer Handbook, MMA SDS) or to defensible audit-derived priors. Every prior is documented in the code comments.

4 — Two operational hypotheses

The model output is the formal posterior. But operationally, there are two competing narratives in the public record. Both are consistent with the same observations through Sunday evening; they predict different futures.

A. Slow cure (OCFA / Covey / McGovern)

"Because of the heavy deluge of water and cooling it, we're allowing it to cure at a slower rate and reducing its overpressure building." — Covey, LAist
"A potential crack in the tank, which could potentially be relieving some of the pressure in there." — McGovern (Interim Chief), ABC7

The deluge + cracks together let polymerization happen at a controlled rate. End state: solid PMMA block inside the tank, or controlled spill into the parking lot pad (the "preferred outcome" per Covey, with sandbag containment). No detonation.

B. BLEVE physics (Picazo, USC)

"Above MMA's boiling point (101 °C / ~213 °F), the pressure starts to increase dramatically, because the liquid phase becomes gas phase." — Picazo, LA Times
"Literal fireballs, and the explosion itself." — Picazo on BLEVE consequences

If the tank reaches its boiling point, pressure rise outpaces any vent. The Synthron 2006 analog tracked operators seeing T climb from 80 °C to 145 °C over 10 minutes before catastrophic failure. The crack may be a precursor to brittle failure rather than a benign vent.

Where the model lands — and where OCFA disagrees. The v4 posterior weights Hypothesis A heavily (~93%) but does NOT rule out B (~7% crossing mass, spread Tue–Thu+). Sunday evening, however, McGovern publicly hedged toward B: "We are not there yet; we need to run this operation tonight." The all-night mission is a real-world Bayesian update — its outcome (announced Monday-morning briefing) will move the posterior materially in one direction or the other. The state-surge composition is also revealing: 785+ personnel mobilized but only 3 hazmat teams and 24 scientists — heavy on law enforcement and shelter ops, light on chemistry mitigation tools. That signals the response posture is "contain and wait" rather than "actively neutralize."

6 — Audit trail

Six independent agent audits have shaped the current model. Each report is preserved in the source corpus.

Round 1 — physics, tank, numbers (2026-05-24)

  • model-audit-2026-05-24.md — found and fixed the solar-phase bug (9-hour offset) and the missing O₂-MEHQ coupling.
  • content-review-2026-05-24.md — caught stale numbers and missing sections.
  • gap-analysis-2026-05-24.md — flagged evaporative cooling as the dominant missing physics, plus 30 other capability gaps.

Round 2 — six-agent deep audit (2026-05-25)

  • physics-audit-2-2026-05-25.md — DIERS-perspective ODE review.
  • tank-audit-2026-05-25.md — process-safety engineer review. Found that the PSV is broken (bulged, stuck).
  • numbers-audit-2026-05-25.md — constant-by-constant calibration check.
  • Bayesian/inference audit — found that hard constraints + bimodal I₀ were driving 99.5% sample waste. Fixing the inference machinery pushed acceptance 0.2% → 68%. (Findings folded directly into v4 — no separate doc.)
  • Polymer kinetics deep dive — found missing thermal Diels-Alder initiation, peroxide pathway, polymer auto-catalysis at the wall skin (Synthron mechanism). Deferred to v5.
  • Fire-service / IC operations — found wetted-coverage 30–55% (not 100%), crew duty cycle ~88%, net IR cooling at night, withdrawal trigger at 150 °F. Folded into v4 as operational-realism multipliers.

The audit history is itself the most useful document on this page — it shows how each refinement moved the posterior. The single biggest swing was the inference fix in round 2: prior versions reported 4.5% holds with 95% near-term crossings. With proper Bayesian inference (soft likelihoods, importance sampling, collapsed redundant priors), the posterior settled at 93% holds — same model, same observations, but the inference machine was previously degenerate.

9 — Caveats & limitations

  • The whole exercise rests on open-source reporting. No interior temperature reading has been publicly released since Friday 9:40 PM PDT (90 °F gauge). Everything since is inferred from the rate-of-rise observation and the Sun 5:50 PM "≥100 °F gauge-pegged" anchor.
  • Threshold prior is centered on Picazo's BLEVE physics (~213 °F) with N(210, 25). The earlier "approximately 100 °F out of control" responder quote is reinterpreted as a caution threshold, not catastrophic.
  • Stratification offset is sampled N(20, 12) °F because the gauge probably reads bulk T but the reaction zone is the hotter top liquid layer. Effective threshold = sampled BLEVE − sampled stratification.
  • Crack-vent magnitude is approximate. No measurement of crack flow rate or geometry exists; the model uses Bernoulli flow on a sampled ~1 cm² area, giving ~5–25 kW depending on T.
  • Operational realism multipliers (coverage_frac ~0.5, duty_cycle ~0.9) come from the fire-service audit. These are honest order-of-magnitude estimates, not measurements.
  • Polymer kinetics not yet at v5. The kinetics audit identified thermal Diels-Alder initiation, peroxide autocatalysis, and polymer auto-catalysis at the wall skin (the actual Synthron 2006 failure mode) as missing. These could shift the posterior modestly toward more crossing mass at high X.
  • Sensitivity script is out of sync with v4 physics. Any sensitivity ranking shown elsewhere uses v3 priors and shouldn't be trusted for v4 conclusions. Rewrite pending.
  • The consequence map's screening formulas (Brode, point-source thermal, Pasquill-Gifford with mild heavy-gas correction) give order-of-magnitude footprints. The audit flagged pool-fire burn rate as conservative (~2× literature) and heavy-gas dispersion correction as possibly non-conservative.
  • This is not operational guidance. Defer to OCFA, EPA, and the responding hazmat teams for any safety-affecting decision.
