2 — The Model (v4)

A lumped-parameter ODE thermal model with chemistry, fouling, vent, and operational realism. Every term traces to a specific finding in one of the six audits. Equations:

ΔT_strat(X) = ΔT_base · (1 + 1.5·X) capped at 60°F v5 DYNAMIC stratification T_rxn = T_bulk + ΔT_strat(X) · 5/9 K reaction at the hot top layer k(T_rxn) = A · exp(−Ea / R·T_rxn) Arrhenius rate g(X) = 1 + 50 · σ((X−0.20)/0.04) · σ((0.85−X)/0.04) Trommsdorff sigmoidal turn-on + vitrification INHIBITED CHANNEL (MEHQ-controlled): inhib(I) = σ((I_crit − I)/0.005) binary-with-smoothing dX_inhib = k · (1−X) · inhib(I) · g(X) v5 CHEMISTRY-BYPASS CHANNEL (escapes MEHQ via C-centered radicals): Ri_th = A_th · exp(−Ea_th/RT_rxn) · [M]² Mayo/Diels-Alder self-init (A_th=1e8, Ea_th=115 kJ) Ri_perox = 2·f_d·k_d·[ROOH], k_d = A_d·exp(−Ea_d/RT_rxn) Hydroperoxide homolysis (A_d=1e14, Ea_d=120 kJ) Ri_poly = 2·f_p·k_p·ρ_HH·X·[M₀]·gate(X)·φ_skin PMMA scission (Synthron) (A_p=1e12, Ea_p=130 kJ; X>0.30) dX_chem = C_th · √(Ri_th + Ri_perox + Ri_poly) · (1−X) · g(X) / [M₀] d[ROOH]/dt = η_ox · Ri_th − k_d · [ROOH] ROOH state (η_ox = I/I₀, max 1) dI/dt = −c_inh · k − 1e−3·I · H(X − 1e−5) MEHQ + O₂ cliff dX_total = dX_inhib + dX_chem FOULING + COOLING + WITHDRAWAL (unchanged from v4): δ(X) = min(3 mm, f_plate · X · m / (ρ_PMMA · A_cool)) CAPPED to physically realistic U_eff(X) = 1 / (1/U_clean + δ(X)/k_PMMA) Q_evap, Q_crack(t), Q_IR, Q_solar(t) as v4 ops = coverage_frac · duty_cycle withdraw = H(T − 150°F) OCFA withdrawal trigger Q_cool = (U_eff · A · (T − T_water) + Q_evap + Q_crack) · ops · (1 − 0.9·withdraw) + Q_IR − Q_solar ΔH_p(X) = 57700 · (1 − 0.07·X) v5: heat-of-poly decreases with X Cp(X) = 1900 − 434·X v5: tracks toward PMMA Cp dT/dt = (Q_gen − Q_cool) / (m · Cp(X)), Q_gen = m · ΔH_p(X) · dX_total State variables: {T, X, I, ROOH} — v5 added ROOH (mol/L) Survival constraints (importance-sampled weights, not hard rejects): HARD reject: effective threshold (BLEVE − ΔT_strat(X_at_crossing)) crossed before hr 75 SOFT weight: Φ((peakF_by_hr_74 − 100 + σ_g) / σ_g) censored-Gaussian gauge likelihood

What's new in v5 vs v4:

  • Mayo / Diels-Alder thermal self-initiation — pure MMA self-initiates via a Diels-Alder dimer that abstracts H to make C-centered radicals. Bypasses MEHQ. Adds a non-zero background radical flux even when inhibitor is fully active. Magnitudes: 4.8e-9 /s at 305 K, 9e-8 /s at 339 K — small alone but seeds peroxide buildup.
  • Peroxide autocatalytic pathway — new state variable [ROOH] in mol/L. Produced during induction (R• + O₂), decomposes thermally with t½ ~10h at 100 °C, minutes at 130 °C. Each ROOH → 2 R• that also bypass MEHQ. This is the autocatalytic clock behind Rohm-Haas 2010 and every reported MMA storage runaway.
  • PMMA backbone scission (Synthron mechanism) — above ~120 °C the polymer skin's head-to-head linkages thermally cleave. Gated on X > 0.30 with smooth turn-on. Evaluated at T_skin (top layer hotter than bulk). Late-stage runaway accelerator.
  • Dynamic stratification — was a fixed per-trajectory sample; now ΔT_strat(X) = ΔT_base · (1 + 1.5·X), capped at 60 °F. Applied to BOTH the threshold check AND the Arrhenius T_rxn — model is now internally consistent (threshold knows the hot spot; reaction happens at it).
  • Cp(X) and ΔH_p(X) tracking — heat capacity shifts toward PMMA's 1466 J/(kg·K) with X; heat of polymerization drops ~7% across full conversion (Schultz & Harborth).

What was new in v4: importance sampling (Bayesian audit fix — biggest single jump in acceptance), I₀ prior collapsed to aged-only, operational realism (coverage × duty), time-gated crack vent, net IR cooling, withdrawal trigger, fouling self-limited at 3 mm, Trommsdorff onset moved to X=0.20.

Calibration honesty. The model is a phenomenological lumped-parameter system, not a first-principles simulation. The pre-exponential A is calibrated to reproduce the observed ~1 °F/hr early rise. All other constants trace to published values (NIST, Polymer Handbook, MMA SDS) or to defensible audit-derived priors. Every prior is documented in the code comments.

4 — Two operational hypotheses

The model output is the formal posterior. But operationally, there are two competing narratives in the public record. Both are consistent with the same observations through Sunday evening; they predict different futures.

A. Slow cure (OCFA / Covey / McGovern)

"Because of the heavy deluge of water and cooling it, we're allowing it to cure at a slower rate and reducing its overpressure building." — Covey, LAist
"A potential crack in the tank, which could potentially be relieving some of the pressure in there." — McGovern (Interim Chief), ABC7

The deluge + cracks together let polymerization happen at a controlled rate. End state: solid PMMA block inside the tank, or controlled spill into the parking lot pad (the "preferred outcome" per Covey, with sandbag containment). No detonation.

B. BLEVE physics (Picazo, USC)

"Above MMA's boiling point (101 °C / ~213 °F), the pressure starts to increase dramatically, because the liquid phase becomes gas phase." — Picazo, LA Times
"Literal fireballs, and the explosion itself." — Picazo on BLEVE consequences

If the tank reaches its boiling point, pressure rise outpaces any vent. The Synthron 2006 analog tracked operators seeing T climb from 80 °C to 145 °C over 10 minutes before catastrophic failure. The crack may be a precursor to brittle failure rather than a benign vent.

Where v5 lands — sharper disagreement with OCFA than v4. v5 puts holds at 99.5% — stronger weight on Hypothesis A than v4's 93%. The added chemistry (Mayo + peroxide + polymer scission + dynamic stratification) tightens the conclusion in the deluge-wins direction because the new radical-source terms produce ROOH that stays bounded under cooling, and the polymer-skin scission is gated on X > 0.30 which most trajectories never reach. Meanwhile McGovern's Sunday-evening walkback ("we are not there yet") explicitly hedged toward B. This disagreement is itself important data. Either (a) OCFA has information we don't (closer-look inspection of the crack, internal sensor readings, specific failure-mode observations) — likely, or (b) the model is too optimistic — possible, given the still-unmodeled Synthron-mode wall-skin runaway happens at T_skin we can't observe. The all-night-mission outcome (Monday-morning briefing) will move the posterior materially.

Key chemistry insight from the brainstorm: The "stuck valve" almost certainly isn't a mechanical failure — it's a polymer plug (MMA self-polymerizing in the outlet jammed it). This implies any other tank penetration is at risk of the same failure mode. Phenothiazine, not MEHQ, is the right inhibitor for the bulk because the bulk is O₂-depleted. Bulk-T interventions dominate shell-T interventions because carbon steel + slow internal convection means shell-cooling barely couples to bulk-T.

6 — Historical-analog calibration

Two MMA-storage incidents in the public record give the model real targets to validate against. Full detail.

Rohm & Haas 2010 (Louisville, KY)

  • 175,000 lb / 17,500 lb released = exactly 10%
  • Vent duration: 13.5 hours (12:30 PM → 2:00 AM)
  • Avg release rate: 0.16 kg/s through PSV
  • DOT-105J car: did NOT rupture, no BLEVE, no fire
  • Vapor-only release; perimeter VOC monitoring went non-detect within 6 hours
  • By analogy with 2008 sister incident at same facility: ~90% of mass likely polymerized to solid PMMA in-situ ("hardened up like plexiglass after about 8 days")

Calibration target: when v5 is run with Rohm-Haas-like parameters (175,000 lb, PSV ~75 psig, ambient ~30 °C), it should reproduce a bounded-pressure plateau that vents ~10% over ~13 hours with no rupture. v5 calibration run pending.

Synthron 2006 (Morganton, NC)

Important correction: Synthron was n-butyl acrylate + benzoyl-peroxide initiator, NOT MMA. Use only as qualitative failure-mode analog.
  • 1,500 gal vertical reactor, 75 psig MAWP
  • Leaked at 23 psig (vs 75 psig MAWP) because only 4 of 18 manway clamps were installed — mechanical containment failure, not chemistry burst
  • Failure mode: secondary CVCE in the BUILDING from vented vapor, not vessel rupture
  • Onset-to-leak: "several minutes" between BPO injection and vent (the "80 → 145 °C in 10 min" line from older audits is NOT in the CSB report)
  • Key lesson: Vent DISCHARGE LOCATION is a first-order safety variable. Rohm-Haas vented to atmosphere = safe; Synthron vented into a building = casualties.
What Rohm-Haas calibration would change: if the v5 model can't reproduce the 10%/13.5h outcome with Rohm-Haas parameters, the kinetics constants need adjustment. Currently the model is calibrated phenomenologically against the Garden Grove observations only. Adding the Louisville test would be a true cross-validation. Deferred to v6.

11 — Caveats & limitations

  • The whole exercise rests on open-source reporting. No interior temperature reading has been publicly released since Friday 9:40 PM PDT (90 °F gauge). Everything since is inferred from one rate-of-rise observation and the Sun 5:50 PM "≥100 °F gauge-pegged" anchor.
  • CRITICAL fact-ledger finding (May 25): the "77 °F Friday morning" reading was exterior drone IR, not interior gauge. The "+13 °F overnight" rise (77→90) was a measurement-category change, not a real heating delta. This means we have ONE real interior anchor (90 °F at hr 30), not two — and the "1 °F/hr" rate-of-rise that's been cited everywhere is computed from an exterior-vs-interior interpolation, not a real heating rate. The model still uses 1 °F/hr as a soft calibration target but this is now flagged as suspect.
  • v5 added three chemistry mechanisms (Mayo, peroxide, polymer scission) with constants from Polymer Handbook + Lin-Hsieh + Denisov. These are at the right order of magnitude but not measured for this specific aged-batch chemistry.
  • Synthron 2006 is NOT an MMA analog — it was n-butyl acrylate + benzoyl peroxide, with mechanical-containment failure (4 of 18 manway clamps) as the proximate cause. Prior audits conflated it with MMA chemistry. Reframed in the calibration section.
  • v5 holds at 99.5% but acceptance dropped to 25% — dynamic stratification rejects more trajectories that creep into the lower effective threshold. ESS efficiency 93% (excellent quality among accepted), but the 75% hard-rejection rate is large and itself informative: most prior-space combinations of parameters are inconsistent with the survival history.
  • Threshold prior is centered on Picazo's BLEVE physics (~213 °F) with N(210, 25). The earlier "approximately 100 °F out of control" responder quote is reinterpreted as a caution threshold, not catastrophic.
  • Stratification offset is sampled N(20, 12) °F because the gauge probably reads bulk T but the reaction zone is the hotter top liquid layer. Effective threshold = sampled BLEVE − sampled stratification.
  • Crack-vent magnitude is approximate. No measurement of crack flow rate or geometry exists; the model uses Bernoulli flow on a sampled ~1 cm² area, giving ~5–25 kW depending on T.
  • Operational realism multipliers (coverage_frac ~0.5, duty_cycle ~0.9) come from the fire-service audit. These are honest order-of-magnitude estimates, not measurements.
  • Polymer kinetics not yet at v5. The kinetics audit identified thermal Diels-Alder initiation, peroxide autocatalysis, and polymer auto-catalysis at the wall skin (the actual Synthron 2006 failure mode) as missing. These could shift the posterior modestly toward more crossing mass at high X.
  • Sensitivity script is out of sync with v4 physics. Any sensitivity ranking shown elsewhere uses v3 priors and shouldn't be trusted for v4 conclusions. Rewrite pending.
  • The consequence map's screening formulas (Brode, point-source thermal, Pasquill-Gifford with mild heavy-gas correction) give order-of-magnitude footprints. The audit flagged pool-fire burn rate as conservative (~2× literature) and heavy-gas dispersion correction as possibly non-conservative.
  • This is not operational guidance. Defer to OCFA, EPA, and the responding hazmat teams for any safety-affecting decision.
