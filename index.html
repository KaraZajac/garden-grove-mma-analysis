2 — The Model

A lumped-parameter ODE thermal model of the tank, integrated forward from the only solid interior anchor (90 °F at hour 30). Six iterations refined the physics, the priors, and the inference machinery.

State variables

  • T — bulk liquid temperature (K)
  • X — conversion fraction [0,1] of MMA → PMMA
  • I — fractional MEHQ inhibitor remaining
  • ROOH — hydroperoxide concentration (mol/L), added v5

Heat-balance terms

  • Q_gen: polymerization exotherm + chemistry-bypass channels (Mayo, peroxide, polymer scission)
  • Q_cool: sensible UA·ΔT, evaporative latent heat from deluge film, crack-vent vapor flow, net IR radiative loss
  • Modifiers: PMMA wall fouling (3 mm cap), operational coverage × duty cycle, withdrawal trigger at 150 °F, dynamic stratification

Inference: importance-sampled approximate Bayesian computation. Hard rejection on "no observed BLEVE crossing before now"; soft censored-Gaussian likelihood on the "gauge pegged ≥ 100 °F at hour 74" observation. 17 sampled priors per trajectory, ~5,000 accepted weighted trajectories per run, ESS efficiency ~93%.

Calibration honesty: the pre-exponential A is phenomenologically tuned. All chemistry constants (Mayo / Diels-Alder, peroxide ROOH dynamics, PMMA backbone scission) trace to published values (Polymer Handbook, Lin-Hsieh, Denisov). Operational realism multipliers (coverage 0.3–0.6, duty cycle 0.7–0.98) come from the fire-service audit. Full math + JS implementation in scripts/montecarlo.js.

Honest read on the validation

The model's headline number (99.5% holds) matched the observed outcome. But the validation depends on the mechanism: the crack-vent that resolved the incident was something the model treated as a stochastic parameter (A_crack ~ log-normal around 1 cm²). The model "got lucky" in the sense that the actual crack flow was high enough to relieve pressure faster than chemistry could build it. A more pessimistic prior on crack-vent flow rate would have flipped the model toward agreeing with OCFA's Sunday-evening hedge — and would also have been defensible at the time.

What the model got right that OCFA's public framing didn't:

  • The 100 °F gauge pegging was not by itself catastrophic — the real physical BLEVE threshold is MMA's boiling point (~213 °F per Picazo), not the responders' "approximately 100" caution figure.
  • The crack discovered Sat night was probably an asset, not a precursor — the v5 priors already weighted the crack-vent term as a stabilizing mechanism.
  • The "slow cure" hypothesis (Covey, LAist) was the right operational frame and the model converged on it independently.

What OCFA got right that the model didn't:

  • Caution under uncertainty. "We are not there yet" was the correct public message even though the model says ~99.5% holds, because the consequence of being wrong is catastrophic.
  • Mechanism specificity. The model treated the crack abstractly; OCFA had to actually go look at it, confirm it wasn't growing, and characterize it as benign before they'd commit to "off the table."

The nine audit agents

Round 1 (Sun 5/24)

  • Physics audit — caught solar-phase 9-hour offset and missing O₂–MEHQ coupling. docs
  • Content review — stale numbers and missing sections.
  • Gap analysis — flagged evaporative cooling as the top missing physics.

Round 2 (Mon 5/25)

  • Physics #2 (DIERS) — deeper ODE review.
  • Tank engineering — PSV is mechanically broken; deluge runoff dominates.
  • Numbers audit — constant-by-constant calibration.
  • Bayesian inference — the highest-impact audit: hard constraints + bimodal I₀ were driving 99.5% sample waste. Inference fix alone moved acceptance 0.2% → 68%.
  • Polymer kinetics — missing Mayo, peroxide, polymer scission.
  • Fire-service ops — wetted-coverage 30–55%, duty-cycle 88%.

Round 3 (overnight): five more agents — Rohm-Haas calibration, Synthron calibration, mitigation brainstorm, fact ledger (280 facts), kinetics implementation spec. All preserved in sources/reference/.

Key meta-finding

The single biggest swing came not from any physics fix but from the Bayesian inference audit. Hard "T ≥ 95 °F at hr 74" constraint + a bimodal I₀ prior with a deterministic-reject tail were dropping acceptance from 38% to 0.2% and producing a degenerate posterior (100% holds, 0 crossings — the model literally couldn't answer the question). Replacing the hard constraint with a censored-Gaussian likelihood and collapsing the unhelpful I₀ tail moved acceptance back to 68% with a meaningful posterior — without changing any physics. Most modeling failures aren't physics failures; they're inference failures.

8 — Caveats

  • Open-source only. No interior temperature reading was publicly released between Friday 9:40 PM (90 °F gauge) and Sunday 5:50 PM (≥100 °F pegged). The Mon 8:13 AM 93 °F reading was the next anchor. Inferences between were stochastic.
  • Fact-ledger correction: the "77 °F Fri morning" reading widely cited in news coverage was exterior drone IR, not interior gauge. The widely-quoted "1 °F/hr rise" was computed from interpolating across measurement categories. The model's calibration used it anyway as a soft target — a real but bounded imprecision.
  • Kinetic constants are representative. A, Ea, and the chemistry-bypass-channel rates (Mayo, peroxide, polymer scission) trace to Polymer Handbook and standard references, but are not measured for this specific aged batch.
  • Validation depends on the crack-vent prior. A more pessimistic A_crack prior would have flipped the v5 posterior toward agreeing with OCFA's "we are not there yet" hedge — also defensible at the time. The validation is real but partly stochastic.
  • v5 cannot reproduce the Rohm-Haas 2010 steady-state PSV-vent regime. v6 calibration showed v5 chemistry produces bimodal outcomes (quiet polymerize OR runaway), not the intermediate. Either the Rohm-Haas regime is genuinely narrow in real chemistry, or our model needs PSV-chatter dynamics that aren't currently captured.
  • Synthron 2006 is NOT an MMA analog — it was n-butyl acrylate + benzoyl peroxide with a mechanical containment failure. Prior audits conflated. Reframed in the fact ledger.
  • Not operational guidance. This is a post-incident retrospective. The audits' value is methodological; numerical estimates are conditioned on a model whose constants are bounded but not measured.
