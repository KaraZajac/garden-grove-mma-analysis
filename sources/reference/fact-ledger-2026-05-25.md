# Cross-references — analytical notes

## What the BLEVE threshold actually is

- MMA BP = 101 °C = 214 °F (Picazo, MIRA, Wiki, SDS): the chemical threshold above which vapor pressure rises dramatically
- Newsantaana's "BLEVE threshold 100 °F" appears unsupported and likely conflates a gauge limit with a chemical limit
- The gauge limit (100 °F) and the boiling point (214 °F) are 114 °F apart
- At 1°F/hr rise, true T to reach BP is on the order of 100+ hours from Sat morning anchor

## What changed Sun 5/24

- ~11:30 AM PT: McGovern "positive intel" framing of crack discovery
- ~5:50 PM PT: gauge pegs at 100 °F (true T unknown)
- ~9:30 PM PT: McGovern walks back to "BLEVE threat not there yet"
- This narrative arc — optimism → instrument blind → walkback — is the dominant Sunday signal

## Single-point-of-failure tank instrumentation

- abc7-temperature-1deg-per-hour: authorities seeking radar technology because only one interior gauge exists
- abc7-firefighters-water-line: only thermal-control mechanism is exterior water (no backup water source until Sat 11 PM disruption forced emergency restoration)
- Sytepoint frames this as the central asset-integrity failure
- DA Spitzer "How could this entity not have any redundancy?" maps directly onto this

- DELIBERATELY NOT USED: foam (ignition risk)
