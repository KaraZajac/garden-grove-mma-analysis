# Methyl Methacrylate (MMA)

**Source:** Wikipedia · **URL:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methyl_methacrylate

## Chemical Structure & Identification

**Methyl methacrylate (MMA)** is an organic monomer with molecular formula **C₅H₈O₂** and molecular weight **100.117 g·mol⁻¹**. The preferred IUPAC name is methyl 2-methylprop-2-enoate. Structure: CH₂=C(CH₃)COOCH₃ — the methyl ester of methacrylic acid.

- **CAS Number:** 80-62-6
- **EC Number:** 201-297-1

## Health & Safety Hazards

- **Eye:** Redness and pain
- **Skin:** Mild irritant; sensitization possible
- **Respiratory:** Nasal irritation at elevated concentrations

## Production Routes

### Cyanohydrin (ACH) Route
Dominant process — >3 billion kg/yr. From acetone + HCN; produces ~1.1 kg ammonium sulfate per kg MMA.

### Methyl Propionate Route
Two-stage: carboalkoxylation of ethylene to methyl propionate, then condensation with formaldehyde. Lucite Alpha process (Singapore, 2008) — minimal waste; can use biomass feedstocks.

### Alternative Routes
- Propionaldehyde / methacrolein
- Isobutyric acid (Atochem/Röhm)
- Methylacetylene (propyne) via Reppe chemistry (99 % yield)
- Direct oxidation of isobutylene
- Methacrylonitrile (MAN) ammoxidation
- Asahi methacrolein esterification

## Applications

~75 % of MMA goes to PMMA acrylic plastics. Other uses:
- MBS (methyl methacrylate-butadiene-styrene) impact modifier for PVC
- Bone cement (orthopedic, ~20 yr service life)
- Raw material for EMA, BMA, 2-EHMA
- Wood impregnation / stabilization
- Adhesives

## Historical

Discovered 1873 by Bernhard Tollens and W. A. Caspary, who observed conversion to a clear hard transparent substance in sunlight. Industrial production began 1931 by Rohm and Haas, founded by Otto Röhm.
