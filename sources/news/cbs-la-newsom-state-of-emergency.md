# California Gov. Newsom declares state of emergency for Orange County chemical leak as DA launches probe into its cause

**Outlet:** CBS Los Angeles · **Published:** Updated May 23, 2026 / 10:39 PM PDT · **URL:** https://www.cbsnews.com/losangeles/news/garden-grove-chemical-leak-plans-orange-county-california-evacuations/

**By:** Austin Turner, Dean Fioresi, Julie Sharp, Matthew Rodriguez, Chelsea Hylton

Governor Gavin Newsom proclaimed a state of emergency in Orange County Saturday following a hazardous chemical leak at GKN Aerospace in Garden Grove. The incident involves an industrial tank containing approximately 7,000 gallons of methyl methacrylate, a flammable epoxy chemical.

## Key situation details

**Temperature crisis:** Tank temperatures rose from 77 degrees Friday morning to 90 degrees Friday night, increasing at roughly one degree per hour. OCFA Incident Commander Craig Covey explained the danger: "the tank goes into a thermal runaway and blows up, affecting the tanks around it."

**Evacuation scale:** More than 50,000 residents were evacuated from Garden Grove, Cypress, Stanton, Anaheim, Buena Park, and Westminster across a one-mile radius zone.

**Chemical risks:** Methyl methacrylate presents respiratory hazards including "lung, skin and eye irritation, nausea and dizziness." USC Chemistry Professor Elias Picazo noted the substance can trigger "runaway" reactions where "heat initiates the reaction, but the reaction releases heat."

## Government response

Governor Newsom stated: "The safety of Orange County residents is the top priority. We are mobilizing every state resource available."

Orange County District Attorney Todd Spitzer launched a criminal investigation, opening an anonymous tipline and urging GKN Aerospace employees to come forward as whistleblowers.

## Evacuation resources

Multiple shelter locations accept pets. Uber provided free rides (code: OCSAFE26) for evacuees. Hotels offered discounted rates, and local restaurants provided free meals for first responders.

## Investigation

No injuries reported. The cause remains under investigation.
