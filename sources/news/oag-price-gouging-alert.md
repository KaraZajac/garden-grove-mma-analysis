# Attorney General Bonta Issues Consumer Alert on Price Gouging Following State of Emergency Declaration in Orange County

**Outlet:** California DOJ / Office of the Attorney General · **Published:** Saturday, May 23, 2026 · **URL:** https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/attorney-general-bonta-issues-consumer-alert-price-gouging-following-state-16

Official consumer protection alert triggered by Governor Newsom's state-of-emergency declaration for Orange County following the Garden Grove MMA tank incident.

## Legal basis

California Penal Code Section 396 prohibits price gouging during a declared state of emergency.

## Price thresholds

- **Existing goods/services:** Price cannot exceed the pre-emergency price by more than 10%
- **New goods/services** (first offered after declaration): Price cannot exceed seller's cost by more than 50%

## Covered categories

Food, emergency supplies, medical supplies, building materials, gasoline, repair and reconstruction services, emergency cleanup, transportation, freight, storage, hotel accommodations, and rental housing.

## Geographic scope

Orange County, California — specifically the City of Garden Grove and surrounding evacuated areas.

## Incident reference

On May 21, 2026, a storage tank containing 5,000–7,000 gallons of methyl methacrylate at GKN Aerospace, Garden Grove began overheating and emitting toxic fumes, prompting mass evacuations and the Governor's state of emergency declaration.

## Reporting

Residents and consumers were directed to report price gouging violations to local law enforcement or file a complaint online with the Attorney General's office.
