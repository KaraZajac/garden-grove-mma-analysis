# Temperature of unstable chemical tank climbs to over 100 degrees, OCFA says

**Outlet:** ABC7 Los Angeles (KABC) live-blog entry 19164358 · **Reported:** Sunday May 24, 2026 (≈1h50m before fetch) · **URL:** https://abc7.com/live-updates/garden-grove-chemical-tank-emergency-leaking-toxic-chemicals-orange-county-will-spill-explode-officials-say/19152918/entry/19164358/

OCFA confirmed the internal gauge on the unstable tank now reads above 100 °F — and that the gauge cannot read any higher, so true interior temperature is unknown. This is a significant escalation past the Sat 90 °F anchor and consistent with the previously reported ~1 °F/hr rise.

## Temperature trajectory

- Friday morning: 77 °F (drone, external)
- Saturday: ~90 °F internal gauge, rising ~1 °F/hr
- Sunday: **>100 °F internal — gauge pegged at maximum**
- Sunday evening exterior shell: ~60 °F
- External cooling target: ~50 °F

## Key new quotes

**OCFA Interim Chief TJ McGovern** (first appearance in our sourcing, replacing/supplementing Covey as briefing lead):
> "What they found was a potential crack in the tank, which could potentially be relieving some of the pressure in there."

**Captain Brian Yau** (new spokesperson):
> "The tank has expanded, and it has cracked. So what is causing it? We need to figure out what is causing the expansion."

## New operational facts

- A crack was discovered during a late-Saturday-night recon mission and may be passively venting pressure.
- No vapors detected by air monitors; no active ground leak.
- Internal temperature instrumentation has hit its physical measurement ceiling — they are flying blind on the bulk temperature.
