# Several Garden Grove Unified campuses closed following chemical leak response

**Outlet:** OCDE Newsroom · **Reported:** Sun May 24, 2026, 9:14 PM PDT · **URL:** https://newsroom.ocde.us/several-garden-grove-unified-campuses-closed-following-chemical-leak-response/

Extended-closure roster issued Sunday evening — districts are now planning around the assumption the incident will run into the school week.

## Schools closed through May 27 (distance learning for last two instructional days)

**Garden Grove USD:** Barker, Bryant, Carver, Enders, Garden Park, Lawrence, Wakeham, Patton (elementary); Bell, Alamitos (intermediate); Pacifica, Rancho Alamitos (high schools).

**Other districts:**
- Magnolia SD: Robert M. Pyles STEM Academy closed
- Savanna SD: **all campuses closed through May 29**
- Westminster SD: Fryberger and Sequoia elementary inaccessible

## Key quotes

**OCFA Interim Chief TJ McGovern:**
> "We're not there yet, but this was a step in the right direction."

**OCFA Division Chief Craig Covey:**
> "We are not giving up. We are working 24 hours a day."

**OC Superintendent Stefan Bean:**
> "School communities … have had to make difficult and fast-moving decisions today."

## Significance

Savanna SD closing **all** campuses through Friday is the most aggressive school-closure footprint to date and implies emergency managers are not expecting full evacuation lifts before week's end.
