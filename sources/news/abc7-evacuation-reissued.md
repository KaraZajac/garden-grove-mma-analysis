# Evacuation orders reissued and expanded, schools closed in Garden Grove as crews work to halt toxic chemical leak coming from tank

**Outlet:** ABC7 Los Angeles (KABC) · **Published:** Friday, May 22, 2026, 11:50 PM PDT · **URL:** https://abc7.com/post/tank-spews-toxic-chemicals-garden-grove-prompting-evacuation-orders/19150431/

## What Triggered This Article

A 34,000-gallon tank at GKN Aerospace in Garden Grove began overheating Thursday at approximately 3:30 p.m., venting toxic methyl methacrylate vapors and activating automatic sprinklers. Evacuation orders were initially lifted Thursday night after exterior cooling appeared to stabilize the tank, then reissued and expanded Friday morning when interior conditions continued to worsen.

## Evacuation / Impact

- Approximately **40,000 residents** affected by mandatory evacuation order
- Zone expanded to a one-mile buffer around the facility
- **13 school campuses** closed

## Craig Covey (OCFA Division Chief) Quotes

- **"There are literally two options left remaining. One, the tank fails and spills a total of about 6,000 to 7,000 gallons of very bad chemicals into the parking lot in that area, or two, the tank goes into a thermal runaway and blows up."**
- **"There is no active gas leak, no plume in the area."**

## Valve / Tank Status

A faulty pressure-release valve on the primary tank prevented crews from:
- Accessing or offloading MMA contents
- Introducing a chemical stabilizer/neutralizer

The valve failure is the central operational obstacle driving the ongoing crisis.

## Chemical

Methyl methacrylate (MMA) — highly toxic, volatile, flammable compound used in plastic/resin manufacturing. Normal storage temperature approximately 50°F. No specific interior temperature readings included in this article.
