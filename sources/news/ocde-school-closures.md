# Several Garden Grove Unified campuses closed following chemical leak response

**Outlet:** Orange County Department of Education (OCDE) Newsroom · **Published:** May 23, 2026 · **URL:** https://newsroom.ocde.us/several-garden-grove-unified-campuses-closed-following-chemical-leak-response/

The Orange County Department of Education published an official newsroom update on school closures and the operational status of the GKN Aerospace MMA tank incident.

## School closures

Eight elementary schools, two intermediate schools, two high schools, plus a preschool facility in Garden Grove Unified School District (GGUSD) were closed due to the evacuation zone. The closures affect campuses falling within or proximate to the chemical hazard perimeter.

## Incident status (as of last update, 5/23/26)

- Tank title: "Compromised tank continues heating as evacuations remain in place"
- Drone monitoring of the tank underway with scans every 10 minutes
- Air quality readings within healthy limits as of most recent report
- Crews transitioned from defensive to offensive mitigation posture
- OCFA described "initial progress toward product removal" but noted valve damage complicated full resolution

## Street closures

Western Avenue and Lampson Avenue remained closed to through traffic. Residents advised to follow posted detour signs.

## Guidance

OCDE directed families to check district communications for reopening timelines. No specific date provided in this release.
