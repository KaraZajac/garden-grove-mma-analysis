# Live Updates: Tank explosion threat "eliminated" — Day 5

**Outlet:** CBS Los Angeles (KCAL) · **Published:** Monday, May 25, 2026 (Day 5 live blog) · **URL:** https://www.cbsnews.com/losangeles/live-updates/california-chemical-leak-orange-county-garden-grove-emergency-day-5/

CBS LA's dedicated Day-5 live blog. Notable for an exact timestamp on the McGovern announcement and a new OCFA spokesperson quote about residual leak risk.

## Timestamped entries

**8:13 AM PDT, Monday:** OCFA Interim Chief TJ McGovern announces:
> "We are happy to report that the threat of a BLEVE is now off the table."

## Temperature & pressure

- Internal tank temperature **dropped from 100°F to 93°F overnight**
- Drop followed discovery of a crack that released pressure
- Covey: "incredibly positive news as we turn the corner on this incident"

## Residual leak risk — new OCFA voice

**OCFA spokesperson Nick Garton** (new name in the briefing):
> "It's still a potential...we're trying to confirm that there's still a liquid form of this chemical."

This frames the open question: is anything still inside the tank that could spill? Officials are unsure whether the contents are still liquid MMA or have already polymerized/vented.

## Operational tempo

- Overnight "all-night mission" Sunday → Monday tested tank pressure
- **Work will resume after dark Monday** — daytime heat presents greater risk to crews and tank
- Pattern: night ops only

## Evacuation status

- ~50,000 residents remain under mandatory evacuation across **six communities**
- ~10 shelters at or near capacity — Red Cross resources strained

## Federal response

- EPA monitoring deployed at **20 locations**
- FEMA coordination support active following Newsom's emergency declaration request
- Trump administration confirmed deployment

## Key context

- Garton is a new OCFA spokesperson name (prior: Huang, Freeman). Adds to the comms roster.
- "Confirm there's still a liquid form" is a significant operational uncertainty — could indicate the tank may have already vented most of its contents through the crack.
- "20 EPA monitoring locations" is a more specific figure than prior coverage.
- Night-only ops cadence formalizes what was previously ad hoc.
