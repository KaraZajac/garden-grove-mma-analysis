# Ongoing updates: Garden Grove chemical incident, west Anaheim evacuations

**Outlet:** City of Anaheim — Official CivicAlert · **Last updated:** May 24, 2026, 6:00 AM PDT · **URL:** https://www.anaheim.net/CivicAlerts.aspx?AID=3203

Official City of Anaheim civic-alert page providing rolling updates on the GKN Aerospace MMA tank incident for the approximately 1,100 Anaheim homes in the mandatory evacuation zone.

## Key status (as of May 24, 2026, 6:00 AM PDT)

"The Garden Grove chemical incident remains ongoing as of Sunday morning with no major changes overnight and the ongoing risk of tank explosion."

"The mandatory evacuations remain in place until further notice."

"There are no updates on when the evacuation orders may be lifted."

"There continues to be no significant chemical release detected in the air."

## Temperature

"The chemical tank temperature has been maintained at approximately 60 degrees Fahrenheit, with optimal temperatures around 50 degrees."

**Note:** The 60°F figure matches previously reported *exterior shell* temperature via unmanned firehoses (CBS/NBC LA Sat 5/23 ~9:40 AM). Interior gauge readings remain at 90°F and rising per OCFA. The Saturday 1:30 PM update noted temperatures were "fluctuating and higher than optimal," consistent with the interior-exterior discrepancy already documented.

## Evacuation zone (Anaheim portion)

Approximately 1,100 Anaheim homes under mandatory evacuation, spanning:
- East–west: Dale Street to Valley View Street (extending into Cypress, past Holder Street)
- North–south: Cerritos Avenue to Ball Road

Incident site approximately 3 miles south of west Anaheim at Western Avenue south of Chapman Avenue in Garden Grove.
