# Garden Grove Residents Remain Evacuated Amid Rising Hazmat Threat

**Outlet:** MyNewsLA.com · **Published:** 2026-05-23 · **URL:** https://mynewsla.com/orange-county/2026/05/23/garden-grove-residents-remain-evacuated-amid-rising-hazmat-threat-3/

## Temperature Readings

- **Friday morning (h≈16):** 77°F interior
- **Saturday (h≈30):** 90°F interior
- Rising rate: approximately **1°F per hour**

## Tank / Chemical Details

- Primary tank: **34,000 gallons** capacity; contains approximately **6,000–7,000 gallons** of methyl methacrylate
- Secondary (adjacent) tank: **15,000 gallons** — OCFA working to neutralize
- Valve status: inoperable; prevents off-load or inhibitor injection

## Evacuation

- Approximately **40,000 residents** under mandatory evacuation order
- Affected cities: Garden Grove, Westminster, Stanton, Anaheim

## OCFA / Craig Covey Quotes

- **"There is no active gas leak, no plume in the area"** — characterizing immediate air quality as of Saturday
- **"Letting this thing just fail and blow up is unacceptable to us"** — Covey on operational priorities

## Timeline

- **Thursday ~3:00 p.m.:** Chemical overheating begins at GKN Aerospace
- **Thursday ~3:30 p.m.:** Initial emergency response
- **Thursday night:** Evacuation orders lifted after initial exterior cooling
- **Friday morning:** Orders reinstated due to worsening interior conditions
- **Saturday:** Governor Newsom declares state of emergency; temperature still rising
