# Evacuations expand with fears of toxic explosion

**Outlet:** LAist · **Published:** May 22, 2026, 1:07 PM · **URL:** https://laist.com/brief/news/garden-grove-residents-asked-to-evacuate-area-toxic-tank-could-explode

LAist coverage of the Friday evacuation expansion, notable for two distinct Covey quotes: the "cure at a slower rate" framing for why continued external cooling might still matter, and the stark acknowledgment that failure is a matter of time.

## Temperature readings

- **Friday morning:** 77 °F
- **Friday night:** 90 °F (direct reading from internal tank gauge)

## OCFA quotes (Craig Covey, Incident Commander)

"One of the concepts we're thinking about is because of the heavy deluge of water and cooling it, we're allowing it to cure at a slower rate and reducing its overpressure building."

"At some point, this is gonna fail, and we're doing our best to figure out the when or how we can prevent it."

## Valve status

A faulty valve discovered early Friday morning caused officials to declare they had exhausted conventional options for securing the tank. The valve failure blocked both product off-load and chemical neutralization, leaving external water cooling as the only active control lever.

## Evacuation details

- Orders reissued and expanded Friday morning after brief Thursday-night lift
- Zone: south of Ball Road / north of Trask Avenue / west of Dale Street / east of Valley View Street
- Approximately 40,000 residents
- Cities: Garden Grove, Cypress, Anaheim, Buena Park, Westminster
