# Garden Grove chemical leak: Tank's internal temperature increasing

**Outlet:** NBC Los Angeles (KNBC) · **Published:** May 23, 2026, 3:07 PM PDT · **URL:** https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/local/garden-grove-gas-leak-temperature/3894210/

The internal temperature of the GKN Aerospace methyl methacrylate storage tank continued to rise on Saturday despite ongoing cooling efforts, according to OCFA Division Chief Craig Covey.

## Temperature readings

| Time | Value | Method |
|------|-------|--------|
| Friday morning | 77 °F | Drone monitoring |
| Saturday morning | 90 °F | Gauge reading via drone |
| Rate of increase | ~1 °F/hr | OCFA measured average |

## OCFA quotes (Craig Covey)

"It's been averaging about a degree an hour, increasing. So, that's the bad news, unfortunately."

"I know there's a lot of anxiety over this. I just need you to understand we are not giving up."

## Tank and valve status

- Pressure-release valve developed a bulge; remained problematic
- Safety valve triggered by temperature increase
- Overhead sprinkler system activated for cooling
- Tank capacity: 34,000 gallons; chemical: methyl methacrylate
- Leak has stopped but situation remains volatile

## Evacuation

Approximately 40,000 residents affected. Four evacuation shelters in operation; one reached capacity by Friday night.
