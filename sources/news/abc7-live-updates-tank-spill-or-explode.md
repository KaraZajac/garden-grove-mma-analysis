# Live updates: Orange County toxic tank on path to spill or explode; experts searching for solutions

**Outlet:** ABC7 Los Angeles (KABC) · **Published:** Last updated Sunday, May 24, 2026 4:49 AM GMT · **URL:** https://abc7.com/live-updates/garden-grove-chemical-tank-emergency-leaking-toxic-chemicals-orange-county-will-spill-explode-officials-say/19152918/

**Authors:** Bianca Buono, Niku Kazori, Mónica De Anda

An unstable chemical tank at the GKN Aerospace facility in Garden Grove presents an imminent threat. Authorities report the tank contains methyl methacrylate and faces two critical scenarios: either releasing thousands of gallons of highly toxic chemical or exploding.

**Temperature status:** The Orange County Fire Authority confirmed that internal temperature readings have been rising approximately one degree per hour since Thursday morning.

**Evacuation scale:** The crisis has displaced 50,000 residents across Garden Grove, West Anaheim, Cypress, Buena Park, and Stanton. The evacuation zone spans from Trask Avenue (north) to Ball Road (south), Valley View Street (east) to Dale Street (west). Police reported a 15% refusal rate as of Friday afternoon.

**Official response:** Governor Gavin Newsom declared a state of emergency in Orange County, enabling additional state resources and shelter sites. The Orange County District Attorney's office established a tip hotline at 714-347-8714 to investigate what caused the material to overheat.

**Support resources:** Uber provided free rides (up to $40) to six designated shelter locations using code "OCSAFE26." Six evacuation shelters were established, with most accepting pets except Golden West College gymnasium.

No injuries have been reported to date.
