# Live updates: Garden Grove tank has potential crack, could 'relieve pressure'

**Outlet:** FOX 11 Los Angeles (KTTV) · **Published:** May 24, 2026 · **URL:** https://www.foxla.com/news/live-updates-garden-grove-evacuation-may-24

FOX LA's Sunday live thread — provides timestamps for the crack announcement and the >100 °F gauge reading from a different outlet than ABC7 (independent corroboration).

## Timestamped anchors

- **11:40 AM PT, Sun May 24** — OCFA Interim Chief TJ McGovern publicly announces potential crack: "a potential crack in the tank, which could potentially be relieving some of the pressure in there." Characterizes overnight inspection as "a successful operation for this emerging incident."
- **5:50 PM PT, Sun May 24** — Tank internal gauge exceeded its 100 °F maximum reading. Risks of catastrophic failure or explosion remain active despite the crack.

## Status

- ~40,000 residents still under mandatory evacuation across Garden Grove, Anaheim, Westminster, surrounding areas.
- Most shelters had reached capacity by 3:00 PM PT.
- Containment barriers remain in place to prevent runoff into storm drains/waterways.

## Why this matters

Independent corroboration (not just ABC7) of (a) the crack discovery, (b) the gauge-pegged >100 °F reading, and (c) the timing of the McGovern briefing window (~11:40 AM PT). Useful for triangulating timeline.
