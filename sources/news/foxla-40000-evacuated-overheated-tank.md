# Garden Grove chemical threat: 40,000 evacuated as overheated tank faces explosion or leak

**Outlet:** FOX 11 Los Angeles · **Published:** Updated May 23, 2026 2:39 PM PDT · **URL:** https://www.foxla.com/news/garden-grove-chemical-threat-overheated-tank-evacuations

**By:** FOX 11 Digital Team

Approximately 40,000 residents in Garden Grove and surrounding Orange County areas face mandatory evacuation orders due to a critical chemical storage tank failure at GKN Aerospace. The 34,000-gallon tank contains methyl methacrylate (MMA), a toxic, highly flammable liquid used in acrylic plastic manufacturing.

## The crisis

The incident began Thursday at 3:30 p.m. when the tank became severely overheated. According to OCFA Division Chief Craig Covey: "There are literally two options left remaining. One, the tank fails and spills a total of about 6,000 to 7,000 gallons of very bad chemicals into the parking lot in that area, or two, the tank goes into a thermal runaway and blows up."

A faulty valve prevents crews from accessing the tank, offloading contents, or introducing a stabilizer. Experts confirm the tank cannot be secured mechanically.

## Temperature escalation

Saturday morning revealed critical deterioration. The internal temperature had risen to 90 degrees from 77 degrees Friday morning, despite cooling efforts with continuous water streams.

## Evacuation zone

The mandatory evacuation encompasses a one-mile radius around the facility:
- **North of:** Trask Avenue
- **South of:** Ball Road
- **East of:** Valley View Street
- **West of:** Dale Street

The entire city of Stanton is also under mandatory evacuation orders.

## Health hazards

Orange County Health Officer Dr. Regina Chinsio-Kwong warned: "It can cause significant irritation in the lungs, the nasal passages, and it can also cause nausea, it can also cause dizziness."

## Infrastructure impact

**Road closures:**
- Beach Boulevard completely closed from Garden Grove Boulevard to Orangewood Avenue
- State Route 22 off-ramps at Beach Boulevard, Knott Avenue, and Valley View Street closed

**School closures:** 14 Garden Grove Unified School District facilities suspended operations

**Community events:**
- Garden Grove Strawberry Festival parade and breakfast canceled
- Voting center and ballot drop boxes within evacuation zone closed
- Los Alamitos Race Course canceled Saturday racing program

**Open:** Disneyland and Knott's Berry Farm remain operational but monitoring conditions.

## State response

Governor Gavin Newsom proclaimed a state of emergency in Orange County, unlocking additional state resources and authorizing use of state-owned properties for shelter.

## GKN Aerospace statement

The company stated it is "working tirelessly with all relevant experts to resolve this situation as safely as possible and in a timely manner" and apologized for disruptions to residents and businesses.

## History

GKN Aerospace settled $900,000 in environmental violations in 2021 for failing to maintain emission records and operating equipment without permits.

## Status

No injuries reported. Air quality remains within healthy limits. Emergency personnel continue monitoring tank temperature with drones while awaiting either structural failure or a controlled leak.
