# California officials warn massive chemical tank will likely spill thousands of gallons or 'blow up'

**Outlet:** Fox News · **Published:** May 22, 2026 6:20pm EDT · **URL:** https://www.foxnews.com/us/california-officials-warn-massive-chemical-tank-likely-spill-thousands-gallons-blow

**Author:** Alexandra Koch

Fire officials in Orange County, California issued urgent evacuation orders Friday as emergency crews worked to contain a critical situation at GKN Aerospace facility in Garden Grove.

**The incident:**
A 34,000-gallon storage tank containing methyl methacrylate (MMA) — a volatile, flammable plastic epoxy used in aerospace manufacturing — began leaking toxic fumes Thursday night. Firefighters implemented cooling measures, and authorities initially allowed residents to return home believing the situation was controlled.

**The crisis escalates:**
By Friday morning, officials determined the tank "is in fact unable to be secured and mitigated," according to Incident Commander Craig Covey.

**Two catastrophic scenarios:**
Officials warned of two potential outcomes:
1. Tank failure spilling 6,000–7,000 gallons of hazardous chemicals into surrounding areas
2. "Thermal runaway" causing explosion, potentially triggering chain reactions in neighboring chemical tanks

Covey stated: "We are setting up these evacuations in preparation for these two options: It fails, or it blows up."

**Response:**
- Two emergency evacuation centers established in Garden Grove and Cypress
- Expanded evacuation zones implemented
- No injuries or fatalities reported as of 1 p.m. local time
- Federal agencies (FEMA, EPA) contacted for assistance
