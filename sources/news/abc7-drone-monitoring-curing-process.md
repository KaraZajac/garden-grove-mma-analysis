# Firefighters seen surrounding tank; drones deployed every 10 minutes for temperature monitoring

**Outlet:** ABC7 Los Angeles (KABC) · **Posted:** May 24, 2026, 7:08 AM GMT (12:08 AM PDT) · **URL:** https://abc7.com/live-updates/garden-grove-chemical-tank-emergency-leaking-toxic-chemicals-orange-county-will-spill-explode-officials-say/19152918/entry/19160210/

Live-blog entry posted overnight May 23–24 covering drone monitoring operations and OCFA's explanation of the heating mechanism.

## OCFA temperature monitoring method

Drones are being deployed every 10 minutes to check tank temperature readings. This supplements the interior gauge and provides near-continuous exterior thermal surveillance.

## OCFA explanation of the heating process

OCFA officials describe the overheating as resulting from the chemical's natural polymerization ("curing") process:

Division Chief Craig Covey characterizes the temperature rise as a "normal curing process" analogous to cement hardening — heat is released as the MMA monomer crosslinks into polymer chains (Trommsdorff/gel effect). This framing aligns with the exothermic free-radical chain mechanism documented in sources/reference/trommsdorff-effect.md.

## Blast zone map

OCFA Division Chief Nick Freeman and the Orange County Health Officer released a potential blast zone map referenced in this entry. Full zone details documented in sources/news/nbc-la-blast-map.md and sources/news/loscerritos-blast-zone-map.md.

## Evacuation status

50,000 residents remain under mandatory evacuation orders as of this entry. No new temperature reading provided beyond the previously established 1°F/hr interior rise.
