# California firefighters say threat of explosion in Orange County chemical tank has been "eliminated"

**Outlet:** CBS Los Angeles (KCAL) · **Published/Updated:** Monday, May 25, 2026, 9:28 AM PDT · **Byline:** Austin Turner, Dean Fioresi, Julie Sharp, Matthew Rodriguez, Chelsea Hylton · **URL:** https://www.cbsnews.com/losangeles/news/california-chemical-leak-orange-county-garden-grove-emergency/

CBS LA's lead Monday-morning report on OCFA's briefing. First major outlet to put exact temperature numbers — 100°F → 93°F — alongside McGovern's BLEVE-eliminated quote.

## Key quotes

**OCFA Interim Chief TJ McGovern:**
> "We are happy to report that the threat of a BLEVE is now off the table."

**Division Chief / Incident Commander Craig Covey:**
> "That is incredibly positive news as we turn the corner on this incident."

## Temperature

- **Internal tank temperature:** decreased from 100°F to 93°F overnight
- Drop attributed to pressure release through the discovered crack

## Crack status

- **One known crack** on tank exterior (discovered Saturday night)
- Crack likely caused by the pressure release itself
- Peeling weather stripping observed on tank — clarified as caused by constant hose-stream cooling, **NOT** an additional crack
- Crack is functioning as an unplanned pressure-relief mechanism

## Overnight operations

Firefighters conducted an "all-night operation" Sunday night into Monday morning to test internal pressure and assess the cracked tank.

## Evacuation status

- More than 50,000 residents remain under evacuation orders
- No disclosed timeline for lifting restrictions

## Key context

- 100°F figure (previously described as "gauge cap") confirmed as the immediate-prior reading before the drop.
- Five-byline group story signals CBS LA treating Monday as the major turn in the incident.
- "Turn the corner" language from Covey directly parallels his prior "preferred outcome is spill" framing — both now displaced by passive depressurization through the crack.
