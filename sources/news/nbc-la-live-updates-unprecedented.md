# Live updates: Firefighters face 'unprecedented' Garden Grove chemical tank crisis

**Outlet:** NBC Los Angeles · **Published:** May 23, 2026 · **URL:** https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/local/live-updates-garden-grove-chemical-tank-crisis/3894268/

**Authors:** Danielle Smith, Karla Rendon, Jonathan Lloyd

**The situation:**
A compromised storage tank at GKN Aerospace facility in Garden Grove contains methyl methacrylate, a flammable liquid. The tank poses serious risk of leaking toxic contents or exploding. The hazard materials response began Thursday night when firefighters were called to address tank problems.

**Evacuation & emergency status:**
Approximately 50,000 residents remain under evacuation orders. Governor Gavin Newsom declared a state of emergency on Saturday.

**Response efforts:**
Orange County Fire Authority personnel are actively working "in harm's way" attempting to neutralize an additional 15,000-gallon tank to eliminate its explosive potential. Authorities are consulting with experts nationwide regarding mitigation options.

**Temperature concern:**
A gauge on the compromised tank (partially submerged in water) showed the internal temperature rising to 90 degrees from 77 degrees Friday morning — a significant increase indicating escalating danger.

**Critical quote:**
The mayor described this as an "unprecedented" emergency situation.
