# OCFA map details potential blast zone in Garden Grove chemical incident

**Outlet:** NBC Los Angeles (KNBC) · **Published:** May 23, 2026 · **URL:** https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/local/garden-grove-chemical-tank-blast-map/3894254/

NBC Los Angeles reporting on the OCFA-released three-tier structural damage map and three-zone chemical hazard map for the GKN Aerospace MMA tank incident.

## Blast / structural damage zones (three-tier)

| Zone | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Inner (severe) | Severe structural damage and significant bodily harm |
| Middle (moderate) | Structural damage less severe than inner zone |
| Outer (light) | Limited but possible structural damage |

## Chemical hazard impact zones (three-zone)

| Zone | Color | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Flammability zone | Red | Areas of flammability; potential for fire or flash fire |
| IDLH zone | Orange | Immediate danger to life and health; injury if inhaled or contacted |
| Odor threshold zone | Yellow | Non-hazardous at odor threshold level |

## Tank / status details

- Tank capacity: 34,000 gallons; chemical: methyl methacrylate (MMA)
- Division Chief Nick Freeman warning: tank could "potentially spill or even explode"
- Interior temperature: 90 °F at time of reporting
- Approximately 40,000 residents under evacuation orders
