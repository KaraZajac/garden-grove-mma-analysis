# Threat of massive explosion at OC chemical tank eliminated; evacuations still in place

**Outlet:** ABC7 Los Angeles (KABC) · **Published:** Monday, May 25, 2026 · **URL:** https://abc7.com/live-updates/garden-grove-chemical-tank-emergency-leaking-toxic-chemicals-orange-county-will-spill-explode-officials-say/19152918/

ABC7's main live-blog headline updated Monday morning following OCFA's briefing: the catastrophic BLEVE risk at the GKN Aerospace facility has been averted.

## Headline status

- **BLEVE (Boiling Liquid Expanding Vapor Explosion) threat:** "now off the table"
- **Tank pressure:** "has released its pressure"
- **Interior temperature:** stabilized at 93°F (down from 100°F+ at gauge cap)
- **Evacuations:** still in place; ~50,000 residents displaced
- **Injuries:** none reported

## Ongoing concerns

Officials caution that a "significantly smaller explosion or leak" remains possible. Areas outside the evacuation zone are described as "completely safe."

## Resident logistics

- Evacuee hotlines: 714-628-7085 and 714-741-5444
- No return-home timeline announced
- Election officials bringing voter registration services to evacuation shelters
- OC DA Todd Spitzer's criminal probe ongoing
