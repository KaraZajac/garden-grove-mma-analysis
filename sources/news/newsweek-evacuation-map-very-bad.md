# California Evacuation Map as Chemical Tank Will Fail or Explode: 'Very Bad'

**Outlet:** Newsweek · **Published:** May 22, 2026 (Updated May 23, 2026 at 06:06 PM EDT) · **URL:** https://www.newsweek.com/garden-grove-california-evacuation-map-chemical-tank-fail-explode-orange-county-11984871

**By:** Anna Skinner and Hollie Silverman

Approximately 40,000 residents in Garden Grove and surrounding Orange County communities face mandatory evacuation orders due to a critical failure at the GKN Aerospace facility. A 34,000-gallon storage tank containing methyl methacrylate — a highly flammable chemical used in plastics manufacturing — began overheating and leaking toxic vapors Thursday afternoon around 3:30 p.m.

## The crisis

Officials have determined the tank cannot be secured through mitigation efforts. According to OCFA Division Chief Craig Covey: "There are literally two options left remaining: One, the tank fails and spills a total of about 6,000 to 7,000 gallons of very bad chemicals into the parking lot in that area. Or two: the tank goes into a thermal runaway and blows up, affecting the tanks around it" that contain additional chemicals.

## Evacuation zones

Mandatory evacuation orders cover areas in Garden Grove, Anaheim, Stanton, and Westminster with these boundaries:
- North of Trask Avenue
- East of Valley View Street
- South of Ball Road
- West of Dale Street

## Health hazards

Methyl methacrylate poses severe risks including respiratory irritation, eye and skin damage, headaches, and potential organ damage from prolonged exposure. The chemical is classified as both highly flammable and hazardous to human health.

## Official response

Governor Gavin Newsom declared a state of emergency Saturday. Los Angeles Mayor Karen Bass confirmed the city faces no risk, though the LAFD is sending hazmat specialists as backup support.
