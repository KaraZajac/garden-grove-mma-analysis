# State of emergency declared in chemical incident in Garden Grove

**Outlet:** NBC Los Angeles · **Published:** May 23, 2026 (Updated 2:11 pm) · **URL:** https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/local/garden-grove-chemical-tank-state-of-emergency/3894233/

**By:** Karla Rendon

Governor Gavin Newsom declared a state of emergency on Saturday in response to a chemical incident in Garden Grove. Approximately 40,000 people are under evacuation orders after a 34,000-gallon tank containing methyl methacrylate — a flammable liquid used in aerospace manufacturing — began leaking at an Orange County aerospace facility.

The chemical has a faulty valve, and emergency crews are preparing for either a spill or potential explosion. OCFA Division Chief Craig Covey stated that officials are developing contingency plans for both scenarios.

The state of emergency proclamation enables affected areas to receive federal emergency support and additional response resources. State-owned properties and fairgrounds are being made available as shelter centers.

## Evacuation centers

Two additional sites were announced, bringing the total to six:

**New locations:**
- Garden Grove Sports & Recreation Center: 13641 Deodara Drive (pets accepted)
- Cypress Community Center: 5700 Orange Avenue

**Existing centers:**
- Ocean View High School: Huntington Beach
- Savanna High School: Anaheim
- John F. Kennedy High School: La Palma
- Freedom Hall: Fountain Valley (reached capacity Friday night)

## Governor's statement

Newsom emphasized: "The safety of Orange County residents is the top priority. We are mobilizing every state resource available to support local responders."
