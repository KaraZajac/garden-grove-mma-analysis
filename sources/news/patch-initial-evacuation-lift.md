# Evacuations issued when holding tank leaks at Garden Grove — OCFA

**Outlet:** Patch (Orange County) · **Published:** Thu, May 21, 2026, 6:22 PM PT (updated 10:47 PM PT) · **URL:** https://patch.com/california/orange-county/evacuations-issued-when-holding-tank-leaks-garden-grove-ocfa

Initial Thursday-evening Patch coverage of the GKN Aerospace tank incident, capturing the first evacuation order and its brief lift by 9 PM PDT.

## Key facts from this article

- **Incident reported:** Reported shortly before 5:45 PM Thursday (OCFA alerted ~3:40 PM PDT per later sources).
- **Tank details:** 34,000-gallon tank at GKN Aerospace facility released methyl methacrylate (MMA), described as "a liquid used to make acrylic plastics."
- **Evacuation zone:** "North of Garden Grove Boulevard, east of Monarch Street, south of Orangewood Avenue and west of Beach Boulevard."
- **Reunification site:** Rancho Alamitos High School, 11351 Dale Street.
- **Cause:** "It's unclear what caused the spill."
- **Initial lift:** Evacuation orders subsequently lifted by approximately 9 PM Thursday (per update).
- **Injuries:** No immediate reports of injuries.
