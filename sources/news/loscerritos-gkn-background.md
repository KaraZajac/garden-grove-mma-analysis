# Little-Known Aerospace Giant GKN at Center of Massive Orange County Hazmat Crisis in Garden Grove

**Outlet:** Los Cerritos Community News · **Published:** May 23, 2026 · **URL:** https://www.loscerritosnews.net/2026/05/23/little-known-aerospace-giant-gkn-at-center-of-massive-orange-county-hazmat-crisis-in-garden-grove/

Background profile on GKN Aerospace and its Garden Grove facility, published as the MMA tank crisis entered its third day.

## GKN Aerospace company profile

- UK-based global aerospace supplier
- 32 manufacturing sites across 12 countries
- Approximately 16,000 employees worldwide
- Garden Grove facility produces acrylic plastics, aircraft windows, and aerospace canopies

## Garden Grove facility

- Address: 12122 Western Ave., Garden Grove
- Products: aircraft canopies, windows, and structural acrylic components
- Uses methyl methacrylate as raw material for acrylic casting

## Regulatory history

- **2018**: California Department of Industrial Relations (DIR) penalized GKN for machinery maintenance and fabrication deficiencies
- No major EPA enforcement actions or large environmental penalties publicly documented for this specific facility
- Note: FOX 11 reporting (foxla-40000-evacuated-overheated-tank.md) referenced a 2021 $900K environmental settlement; the Los Cerritos article found no EPA record confirming this for the Garden Grove site specifically

## Incident context

Tank temperature stabilized at approximately 61 °F by Friday evening per initial reports, but subsequently rose to 90 °F by Saturday morning. Covey: "We have a tank that is actively in crisis. This thing is going to fail. We don't know when."
