# Temperature in chemical storage tank has increased, not cooled, OCFA says

**Outlet:** ABC7 Los Angeles (KABC) · **Entry URL:** https://abc7.com/live-updates/garden-grove-chemical-tank-emergency-leaking-toxic-chemicals-orange-county-will-spill-explode-officials-say/19152918/entry/19157063/ · **Blog last updated:** Sunday, May 24, 2026, 7:18 AM GMT

Live-blog entry in which OCFA reversed its earlier "temperature stabilized" characterization. Interior gauge confirmed the bulk liquid had actually risen, contradicting the apparent exterior-shell stabilization.

## Temperature readings

- **Drone exterior reading (Friday):** 77 °F
- **Internal gauge reading:** 90 °F (interior bulk)
- **Rate of rise:** approximately 1 °F per hour since Thursday morning

## Key context

- This entry directly contradicts entry/19153168 ("OCFA says temperature stabilized"), which reflected only exterior shell conditions.
- Demonstrates the operational blind spot: exterior spray cooling masked continued interior heating.
- Interior gauge data is the primary source for the h=30 timeline anchor (Fri 5/22 ~9:40 pm PDT, 90 °F).
- 50,000 residents remained under mandatory evacuation orders.

## OCFA quotes (Craig Covey)

"Letting this thing just fail and blow up is unacceptable to us."

Officials noted they were "allowing the tank to cure at a slower rate and reducing its (building) overpressure."
