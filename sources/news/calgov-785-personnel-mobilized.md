# California mobilizes 785+ emergency personnel in OC hazmat response

**Outlet:** Office of the Governor · **Reported:** Sun May 24, 2026 · **URL:** https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/05/24/california-mobilizes-785-emergency-personnel-in-orange-county-hazmat-response/

First official accounting of the state-level surge. Notably, the breakdown is heavy on law enforcement and sheltering — not chemical mitigation specialists.

## Personnel breakdown (785+)

- **421** local law enforcement officers (mutual aid)
- **170** firefighters
- **71** Cal OES staff coordinating (State Operations Center 24/7)
- **43** sheltering and public-health staff
- **30** CHP officers (traffic/evac)
- **24** state scientists / toxicologists / engineers (CalEPA, CARB, OEHHA, Water Boards)
- **22** State Parks rangers and lifeguards
- **12** Caltrans maintenance trucks
- **3** specialized hazmat teams
- **20** real-time air-monitoring devices

## Newsom quote

> "California is mobilizing every available resource to protect impacted communities and support local response efforts. We are working hand-in-hand with local officials and emergency personnel to keep people safe, support evacuations and sheltering operations, monitor environmental conditions, and ensure communities receive timely and accurate information."

## Analytical note

The release lists no chilled-water, CO₂, foam, robotic-platform, or active-suppression assets. State environmental presence is monitoring (air) rather than mitigation (chemistry). Operational tank work remains OCFA + GKN industrial crews + contracted hazmat specialists.
