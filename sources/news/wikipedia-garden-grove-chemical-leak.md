# Garden Grove chemical leak

**Outlet:** Wikipedia · **Published:** May 24, 2026 (continually updated) · **URL:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garden_Grove_chemical_leak

## Incident overview
On May 21, 2026, at approximately 3:40 p.m. PDT, a hazardous materials incident occurred at a GKN Aerospace facility in Garden Grove, California. A 34,000-gallon tank containing roughly 7,000 gallons of methyl methacrylate (MMA) — a flammable chemical used in plastic manufacturing — began overheating and leaking.

## Key details

**Chemical properties:** MMA is volatile and can irritate eyes, skin, and lungs while potentially causing respiratory and nervous system damage.

**Temperature crisis:** Internal tank readings showed a concerning increase from 77°F (25°C) on the morning of May 22 to 90°F (32°C) by that evening, with authorities estimating a 1°F hourly rise.

**Evacuation scope:** Over 44,000 residents across nine square miles were evacuated, affecting Garden Grove, Anaheim, Buena Park, Cypress, Stanton, and Westminster.

## Company background
GKN Aerospace, a UK-based manufacturer, has operated the Garden Grove facility since 1993 on 15.5 acres, producing military canopies and aircraft windows. The company faced previous regulatory violations in 2018 and 2021, including environmental and maintenance infractions.

## Response actions
- Governor Gavin Newsom declared a state of emergency on May 23
- Multiple evacuation shelters established across Orange County
- Schools closed or relocated classes
- Emergency updates translated into Vietnamese for the significant Vietnamese-American population in Little Saigon
- Uber offered free rides; Planet Fitness opened facilities to evacuees

The situation remained critical with authorities consulting experts nationwide on prevention strategies.
