# 40,000+ Garden Grove Area Residents Remain Evacuated Amid Hazmat Threat

**Outlet:** MyNewsLA.com · **Published:** May 23, 2026 · **URL:** https://mynewsla.com/orange-county/2026/05/23/40000-garden-grove-area-residents-remain-evacuated-amid-hazmat-threat-2/

Saturday update from MyNewsLA covering the second full day of the GKN Aerospace MMA tank evacuation.

## Evacuation status

- 40,000+ residents remain evacuated
- Evacuation orders first lifted Thursday night; reinstated Friday morning "due to changing conditions"
- Approximately 15% of evacuated residents refusing to comply
- Location: 34,000-gal tank at GKN Aerospace, 12122 Western Ave., Garden Grove

## OCFA (Covey) key quote

"The tank that is in the biggest crisis is in fact unable to be secured and mitigated. There are literally two options left remaining — one, the tank fails and spills...or two, the tank goes into a thermal runaway and blows up."

## Operational timeline

- Initial response: ~3:30 p.m. Thursday, May 21
- Covey received worsening intelligence update at 4 a.m. Friday, May 22
- Drones deployed to monitor tank temperatures remotely

## Civic and community impact

- **Vote centers / ballot drop boxes:** 1 vote center and 3 ballot drop boxes temporarily closed; 39 vote centers remained open
- **Garden Grove Strawberry Festival:** Annual parade and community breakfast canceled due to evacuation zone overlap
- **School closures:** 15 campuses closed

## Containment efforts

Containment barriers deployed to prevent environmental contamination of storm drains and waterways.
