# Garden Grove toxic chemical tank with faulty valve will likely leak or explode, fire officials say

**Outlet:** NBC Los Angeles · **Published:** Published May 21, 2026; Updated May 23, 2026 at 9:47 am · **URL:** https://www.nbclosangeles.com/news/local/garden-grove-hazmat-response-leak/3893550/

**Authors:** Danielle Smith and Jonathan Lloyd

A hazardous materials emergency unfolded at GKN Aerospace in Garden Grove involving a 34,000-gallon tank containing methyl methacrylate, a flammable liquid used in aerospace manufacturing.

**The chemical:** Methyl methacrylate is a colorless, volatile, flammable liquid that can irritate skin, eyes, and respiratory passages. The tank typically stores the chemical at 50 degrees but reached at least 95 degrees during the incident.

**The crisis:** The tank developed a faulty pressure-release valve with a visible bulge. Orange County Fire Authority Division Chief Craig Covey stated: "The tank that is in the biggest crisis is in fact unable to be secured and mitigated...the tank fails and spills...6,000 to 7,000 gallons of very bad chemicals into the parking lot" or explodes, affecting surrounding tanks.

**Temperature status:** By Friday afternoon, active cooling efforts had stabilized the tank's temperature at approximately 60 degrees.

**Evacuations:** Approximately 40,000 people faced mandatory evacuation orders covering areas north of Garden Grove Boulevard, east of Springdale Avenue, west of Dale Street, and south of Orangewood Avenue.

**Emergency resources:** Four evacuation centers remained open, with an emergency hotline at 714-741-5444.
