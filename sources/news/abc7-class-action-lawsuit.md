# Class action lawsuit filed against GKN Aerospace amid chemical leak crisis in Garden Grove

**Outlet:** ABC7 Los Angeles (KABC) · **Entry published:** May 24, 2026, ~4:32 AM GMT · **URL:** https://abc7.com/live-updates/garden-grove-chemical-tank-emergency-leaking-toxic-chemicals-orange-county-will-spill-explode-officials-say/19152918/entry/19160256/

Live-blog entry reporting the class-action lawsuit filed against GKN Aerospace by evacuated residents.

## Lawsuit details

- **Law firms:** X-Law Group P.C. and Presidio Law Firm (confirmed via Wikipedia: filed 2026-05-23)
- **Lead attorney:** Filippo Marchino
- **Named plaintiffs:** Two (expected to expand given 50,000 evacuees)
- **Estimated damages:** Potentially hundreds of millions to billions of dollars

## Key quote (Filippo Marchino)

"When you're storing highly toxic, highly volatile chemical compounds, you take on a responsibility."

## Key context

- KTLA filed a story on this lawsuit (https://ktla.com/news/orange-county/class-action-lawsuit-filed-chemical-leak-crisis-garden-grove/) which was denied HTTP 403 on prior attempts.
- Wikipedia's Garden Grove chemical leak entry had noted the class action but without attorney details or damage estimates.
- This is a legal-liability development; no operational impact on tank temperature or evacuation status.

## Shelter capacity (same overnight window)

- Nine shelters operational, most at or near capacity.
- Red Cross providing meals and supplies.
- Resident frustration with unresolved timeline.
