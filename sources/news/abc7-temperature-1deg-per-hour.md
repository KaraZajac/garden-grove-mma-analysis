# Temperature of unstable tank is increasing 1 degree per hour, officials say

**Outlet:** ABC7 Los Angeles (KABC) · **Entry last updated:** Sunday, May 24, 2026, 7:18 AM GMT · **URL:** https://abc7.com/live-updates/garden-grove-chemical-tank-emergency-leaking-toxic-chemicals-orange-county-will-spill-explode-officials-say/19152918/entry/19158460/

Live-blog entry confirming the 1 °F/hr rate of rise from the tank's internal gauge. Also reports that authorities are seeking radar technology capable of measuring the interior of the sealed tank — a significant operational detail about the limited internal visibility problem.

## Temperature readings

- **Friday morning:** 77 °F (drone measurement)
- **Friday night / Saturday:** 90 °F (internal gauge)
- **Rate of rise:** ~1 °F per hour (confirmed)

## OCFA quotes (Craig Covey)

"Worst-case scenario" — worst he has faced in his 32-year career.

"Think about an ice cube, right? When you freeze water, it starts from the outside in, until the end is solid. That's what's going on here." (Explaining why external cooling does not directly reflect internal bulk temperature.)

"Letting this thing just fail and blow up is unacceptable to us."

Officials described allowing the tank to "cure at a slower rate and reducing its (building) overpressure."

## Key new fact

Authorities were **seeking radar technology** to measure conditions inside the sealed tank. This confirms that as of this entry, OCFA had no direct real-time interior temperature visibility beyond the single internal gauge, and were actively looking for additional measurement methods.

## Context

- 50,000 residents under evacuation orders
- No injuries reported
- Governor Newsom declared a state of emergency in Orange County
- Air monitors detecting no MMA plume
