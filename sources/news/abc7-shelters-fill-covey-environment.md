# Orange County shelters fill as residents evacuate due to tank crisis

**Outlet:** ABC7 Los Angeles (KABC) · **Entry last updated:** Sunday, May 24, 2026, 7:18 AM GMT · **URL:** https://abc7.com/live-updates/garden-grove-chemical-tank-emergency-leaking-toxic-chemicals-orange-county-will-spill-explode-officials-say/19152918/entry/19160217/

Live-blog entry from May 24 (immediately preceding entry/19160256 the class-action entry) reporting that 9 evacuation shelters are at or near capacity, and surfacing a new Covey quote focused on environmental runoff containment — indicating OCFA has begun planning for the secondary scenario of a controlled or uncontrolled spill and protecting waterways.

## Shelter status

- 9 shelters established across Orange County
- Most at or near capacity
- Red Cross providing meals and supplies

## Temperature / monitoring

- Rising approximately 1 °F/hr since Thursday
- Internal gauge + drone surveillance every 10 minutes

## OCFA quotes (Craig Covey)

"The next phase has been preventing further movement of anything. If it comes out of the tank into the waterways and possibly out to the ocean, we want to protect the environment."

Regarding the chemical process: "So we think it's doing its normal curing process…That's part of the chemical process. It creates heat."

## OCFA spokesperson (Wayhowe Huang)

"Resources with all different agencies…We're all working around the clock, trying to find the best solution."

## Key details

- Evacuation zone: north of Trask Avenue, south of Ball Road, east of Valley View Street, west of Dale Street; includes portions of West Anaheim, Cypress, Buena Park, and entire city of Stanton
- ~15% refusal rate as of Friday afternoon
- 50,000 residents evacuated; no evacuation lift announced
- No injuries reported
- Cause still unknown as of this entry
