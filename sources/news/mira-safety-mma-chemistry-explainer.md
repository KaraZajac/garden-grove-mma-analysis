# Garden Grove Chemical Tank Leak: What's Happening

**Author:** Colin Ma · **Outlet:** MIRA Safety blog · **Published:** May 24, 2026 · **URL:** https://www.mirasafety.com/blogs/news/garden-grove-chemical-tank-leak

PPE-industry analysis with the cleanest explicit statement of the **MEHQ (4-methoxyphenol) inhibitor depletion mechanism** that I found in open coverage. Useful as the named-chemistry source for the cause hypothesis.

## Polymerization mechanism (explicit)

> "Once inhibitor protection drops, heat accelerates polymerization, and polymerization accelerates heat. That feedback loop is the thermal runaway authorities are racing."

MEHQ is named explicitly as the standard MMA stabilizer. Implicitly: any cause that drains MEHQ (heating, extended storage past inhibitor half-life, oxygen depletion in a sealed N₂-blanketed tank, contamination with a free-radical initiator like a peroxide) could trigger the cascade.

## MMA physical properties

- Boiling point: 100.5 °C (~213 °F)
- Vapor density: 3.45 (heavy — pools low, follows terrain)
- Respirator class: EN 14387 Class A (organic vapors, BP > 65 °C)

## Historical incidents named

- **Phillips 66 Pasadena (1989)** — flagged as "closest historical analog" for thermal runaway in a populated corridor.
- East Palestine OH (2023), Bhopal (1984), West Fertilizer (2013).
- **Notably absent:** Synthron 2006 and Rohm & Haas 2010, the two most chemically and operationally similar MMA/acrylate-polymerization runaway tank incidents. No source in the news pool we've collected has cited those.

## Mitigation framing

Only generic: water dousing to slow reaction; reference to ice-cube/outside-in solidification model (matches Covey's Sat quote). No technical discussion of alternative inhibitors (DPPH, TEMPO, phenothiazine, hydroquinone) or cryogenic options.
