# OCFA Crews Attend Leak at GKN Aerospace in Garden Grove

**Outlet:** MyNewsLA.com · **Published:** May 22, 2026 · **URL:** https://mynewsla.com/orange-county/2026/05/22/ocfa-crews-work-to-stabilize-leak-at-gkn-aerospace-in-garden-grove-3/

**Author:** Contributing Editor

Orange County Fire Authority crews responded to a chemical leak at GKN Aerospace in Garden Grove on Thursday afternoon. An industrial chemical overheated inside a 34,000-gallon storage tank around 3:30 p.m., releasing vapors into the air.

The chemical involved was methyl methacrylate, described as "a volatile and flammable chemical" used in manufacturing acrylic plastics. Fire crews began spraying water on the tank to cool it down as buildings across a wide area were evacuated.

Initial evacuation orders were lifted at 8:40 p.m. Thursday, but were reissued Friday morning. According to the OCFA, "an inoperable valve on the tank has created additional operational challenges, preventing complete mitigation at this time."

## Key details

**Evacuation zone:** North of Garden Grove Boulevard, east of Springdale Avenue, west of Dale Street, and south of Orangewood Avenue

**Evacuation centers:**
- Stanton City Hall, 7800 Katella Ave.
- Garden Grove Sports & Recreation Center, 13641 Deodara Drive

**School closures:** 15 Garden Grove Unified School District campuses, including elementary schools, intermediate schools, and high schools

**Status:** No injuries reported; cause of tank overheating remains unclear
