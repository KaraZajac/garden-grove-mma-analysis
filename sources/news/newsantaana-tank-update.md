# Latest updates on the Garden Grove chemical tank emergency

**Outlet:** New Santa Ana · **Published:** May 23, 2026 · **URL:** https://newsantaana.com/latests-updates-on-the-garden-grove-chemical-tank-emergency/

**Note on flash point figure:** This article states MMA flash point as 50 °F. The actual MMA flash point is approximately 2 °C (35.6 °F) per SDS data and NFPA. The figure in this article appears to be an error and should not be used as a technical reference.

## Temperature readings

- Internal tank temperature: 90 °F (as of report date)
- Prior reading: 77 °F
- Rate of increase: ~1 °F/hr
- Critical BLEVE threshold cited: 100 °F

## Valve mechanism — unique detail

The pressure-relief valve became inoperable because the methyl methacrylate auto-polymerization reaction converted the liquid into a thick, sticky gel that entered the valve's tight tolerances, essentially sealing the mechanism. Intense heat and pressure also physically warped the tank structure, mechanically seizing the valve further. This is a distinct explanation not captured in mainstream wire coverage.

## Secondary tank

15,000-gal adjacent tank being treated with neutralizer by OCFA crews working "in harm's way."
