# Firefighters seen surrounding tank at center of chemical crisis in OC; water line issues overnight

**Outlet:** ABC7 Los Angeles (KABC) · **Posted:** May 24, 2026, 6:08 AM GMT (May 23, 11:08 PM PDT) · **URL:** https://abc7.com/live-updates/garden-grove-chemical-tank-emergency-leaking-toxic-chemicals-orange-county-will-spill-explode-officials-say/19152918/entry/19160048/

Live-blog entry covering Saturday-night operational activity at the GKN Aerospace tank, notably reporting a water-line disruption that required water department personnel to re-enter the exclusion zone.

## Key operational details

- AIR7 aerial video showed firefighters surrounding the tank at approximately **10:40 p.m. Saturday (May 23)**.
- Fire crews had otherwise been maintaining standoff distance from the property given the explosion/spill risk.
- Officials stated the firefighter presence was "part of a larger effort to try to find any solution they can to stop a potential disaster."

## Water line issue

> "Firefighters were again seen outside the tank. In that instance, officials said they had to usher members of the water department onto the property because there were **issues with the water line** being used to try to cool the tank."

This is the first reported disruption to the exterior cooling water supply. Cooling via unmanned firehoses/deluge is currently the sole active thermal-control mechanism; any break in water flow would remove the only lever available to slow interior temperature rise. Duration of the interruption was not specified.

## Temperature readings

No new temperature reading in this entry. Interior rise of approximately 1 °F/hr remains the last reported rate.

## Evacuation status

50,000 residents remain under mandatory evacuation orders as of this entry. No changes to the evacuation zone boundary reported.
