# Garden Grove Blast Zone Map Shows Homes, School Near GKN Aerospace Chemical Threat

**Outlet:** Los Cerritos Community News · **Published:** May 23, 2026 · **URL:** https://www.loscerritosnews.net/2026/05/23/garden-grove-blast-zone-map-shows-homes-school-near-gkn-aerospace-chemical-threat/

**Author:** Brian Hews

An Orange County Fire Authority operations map reveals that residential neighborhoods, apartment buildings, and an elementary school campus sit within projected damage zones surrounding an ongoing chemical emergency at GKN Aerospace in Garden Grove.

## Key details

**Location:** The aerospace facility is positioned near Western Avenue between Chapman Avenue and Lampson Avenue.

**Chemical threat:** Officials are managing a volatile chemical tank containing methyl methacrylate that "could either rupture and spill thousands of gallons of chemicals or potentially explode if crews cannot stabilize temperatures inside the compromised tank."

**Affected areas:**

The severe damage zone borders include Belgrave Avenue, Bently Avenue, Devonwood Avenue, Laurelhurst Avenue, and portions of Anaconda Avenue near the Stanton Industrial Lead corridor.

The moderate and light damage zones extend toward Santa Gertrudes Avenue, Chapman Avenue, Lampson Avenue, and neighborhoods west of Beach Boulevard, including portions of Georgia Street, Feldgate Street, and Santa Rosalia Street.

**Schools:** Wakeham Elementary School falls within the projected moderate damage area.

**Evacuations:** Mandatory evacuations affect approximately 40,000 residents while crews work to prevent what officials describe as a potentially catastrophic event.
