# OCFA says temperature of toxic chemical tank in crisis has stabilized

**Outlet:** ABC7 Los Angeles (KABC) · **Entry last updated:** Sunday, May 24, 2026, 7:18 AM GMT · **URL:** https://abc7.com/live-updates/garden-grove-chemical-tank-emergency-leaking-toxic-chemicals-orange-county-will-spill-explode-officials-say/19152918/entry/19153168/

Live-blog entry in which OCFA Division Chief Craig Covey initially reported the tank temperature had been maintained/stabilized through exterior water curtain efforts. Subsequent interior gauge readings (entry/19157063) revealed the exterior stabilization masked continued interior heating — the exterior shell was cool while the bulk liquid interior remained at 90 °F and rising.

## OCFA quotes (Craig Covey)

"The positive news is we have been able to keep the tank temperature maintained."

"We've been continuing our water curtain on it, and our active monitoring has shown that the temperature of it has stabilized."

"That was what we were handed: a leaking tank or a tank that blows up."

## Key context

- Statement reflects external drone/IR monitoring showing a stable exterior shell temperature
- Interior gauge entry (entry/19157063) later confirmed the internal bulk had risen from 77 °F to 90 °F, contradicting the apparent exterior stabilization
- Demonstrates the critical distinction between exterior shell cooling and interior bulk temperature that defines the operational challenge
- 50,000 residents remained under evacuation orders
- ~15% refusal rate reported by police as of Friday afternoon
