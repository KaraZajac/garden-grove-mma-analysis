# 40,000 under evacuation orders after chemical tank leak in Southern California

**Outlet:** PBS NewsHour (Associated Press wire) · **Published:** May 23, 2026, 3:49 PM EDT · **URL:** https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/40000-under-evacuation-orders-after-chemical-tank-leak-in-southern-california

**By:** Olga R. Rodriguez, Claire Rush, Hannah Schoenbaum (Associated Press)

Authorities in Southern California urgently worked to prevent potential failure of a storage tank containing between 6,000 and 7,000 gallons of methyl methacrylate that began leaking Thursday at an aerospace plastics facility in Garden Grove, Orange County.

The hazardous chemical overheated and started venting vapors into the air. Officials warned the tank could either crack and release chemicals onto the ground or explode. As many as 40,000 residents received evacuation orders across Garden Grove and parts of five neighboring cities: Cypress, Stanton, Anaheim, Buena Park, and Westminster.

Orange County Fire Authority Division Chief Craig Covey stated: "This thing is going to fail, and we don't know when."

The incident occurred at GKN Aerospace, which manufactures parts for commercial and military aircraft. No injuries or deaths were reported. By late Friday, authorities maintained the tank's temperature, buying time to develop a solution. Crews established containment barriers with sandbags to prevent toxic chemical from reaching storm drains, creeks, or the ocean.

The chemical exposure poses health risks including respiratory issues, eye irritation, nausea, and headaches. Local Vietnamese television stations provided translated updates to help community members understand evacuation directives.

GKN Aerospace said specialized hazardous material teams are assessing the situation: "There are no reports of injuries at this time and our priority remains the safety of our employees, responders, and the surrounding community," a spokesperson said in an emailed statement.
