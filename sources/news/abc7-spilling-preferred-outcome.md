# Authorities say Garden Grove chemical tank spilling is the preferred outcome

**Outlet:** ABC7 Los Angeles (KABC) · **Entry published:** May 22, 2026, 11:56 PM GMT · **URL:** https://abc7.com/live-updates/garden-grove-chemical-tank-emergency-leaking-toxic-chemicals-orange-county-will-spill-explode-officials-say/19152918/entry/19152879/

Live-blog entry clarifying that of the two failure modes, a spill is the "best-case scenario" relative to an explosion.

## Key Covey quote on failure modes

OCFA Division Chief Craig Covey:

> "One, the tank fails and spills a total of about 6,000 to 7,000 gallons of very bad chemicals into the parking lot in that area, or two, the tank goes into a thermal runaway and blows up."

The spill is described as the "best-case scenario" compared to the explosion risk. OCFA has established a containment barrier to prevent chemical material from entering storm drains or waterways in the event of a controlled spill.

## Air quality

Covey confirmed: "There is no active gas leak, no plume in the area" at the time of this entry.

## Evacuation count

Approximately 40,000–50,000 residents affected.

## Valve status

A faulty valve on the compromised tank prevented crews from accessing, off-loading, or introducing stabilizers.

## Facility address

GKN Aerospace facility, 12122 Western Ave., Garden Grove, CA.

## Context

This entry provides the clearest Covey framing of the "preferred" outcome — a spill — versus thermal runaway/explosion. The "best-case scenario" characterization is analytically important: it confirms OCFA accepts MMA release to the parking lot as a controlled-failure path, provided containment barriers hold.
