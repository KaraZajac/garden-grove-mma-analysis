# What is methyl methacrylate? Chemical inside Garden Grove tank and its effects on humans, animals

**Outlet:** ABC7 Los Angeles (KABC) · **Published:** Saturday, May 23, 2026 1:24 AM · **URL:** https://abc7.com/post/what-is-methyl-methacrylate-toxic-chemical-leak-garden-grove-tank-center-hazmat-crisis/19152928/

**Author:** Denise Dador

A hazmat crisis in Garden Grove involves an estimated 7,000-gallon leak of methyl methacrylate (MMA) from a large storage tank. Officials describe the substance as highly toxic and flammable with explosive potential.

### Chemical properties & uses
MMA is utilized in manufacturing shatter-resistant acrylic glass, paints, adhesives, resins, and plastics. The vapor is heavier than air, meaning it settles closer to ground level if released.

### Health effects
Exposure occurs through inhalation or direct contact. Short-term exposure irritates skin, eyes, and the respiratory system. According to Division Chief Nick Freeman, the substance "can start off very mild, but it can progress to a point where, yes, you would probably require hospitalization."

Higher concentrations cause severe respiratory distress. Orange County Deputy Health Officer Dr. Regina Chinsio-Kwong emphasized that severe exposure may necessitate hospitalization and evacuation compliance.

Health impacts depend on exposure level and duration, potentially affecting multiple organs with sudden contact or causing longer-term effects from prolonged exposure. MMA is not currently classified as carcinogenic.

### Animal & environmental impact
The chemical can affect animals, including birds, though species-specific impacts vary. Air quality remained within normal limits as of Friday afternoon, with ongoing monitoring continuing.
