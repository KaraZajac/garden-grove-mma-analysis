# Hazardous chemical leak forces second round of evacuations in Garden Grove, Stanton

**Outlet:** FOX 11 Los Angeles · **Published:** May 21, 2026 11:42 PM PDT · **URL:** https://www.foxla.com/news/hazardous-materials-alert-forces-evacuations-garden-grove-before-orders-lifted

**By:** FOX 11 Digital Team, Gigi Graciette, and CNS staff

A hazardous materials incident at GKN Aerospace on Western Avenue in Garden Grove has triggered mandatory evacuation orders for residents on the Garden Grove–Stanton border. The facility experienced a leak from a 34,000-gallon tank containing methyl methacrylate (MMA), a self-heating plastic epoxy used in aircraft manufacturing.

## Key details

**The chemical:** Methyl methacrylate (MMA) is "a plastic epoxy used to create parts for the aerospace industry" that generates its own heat, prompting firefighters to implement cooling measures.

**The problem:** A valve on the tank is inoperable, allowing the chemical to continue leaking into the air. However, OCFA Chief Craig Covey stated: "there is no active gas leak, no plume in the area" while cautioning residents about potential spill or explosion risks.

**Evacuation zones:** Residents between Trask Avenue and Ball Road, Valley View Street and Dale Street must evacuate immediately.

**School closures:** Fifteen Garden Grove Unified School District campuses closed, including Pacifica High School, Rancho Alamitos High School, and multiple elementary schools.

**Resources:** A public information hotline operates at 714-628-7085. Evacuation centers opened at Stanton City Hall (7800 Katella Ave.) and Garden Grove Sports & Recreation Center (13641 Deodara Drive).
