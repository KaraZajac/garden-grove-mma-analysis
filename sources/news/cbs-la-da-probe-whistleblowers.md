# Orange County DA launches probe into toxic chemical leak in California: "We are not getting satisfactory answers"

**Outlet:** CBS Los Angeles (KCAL) · **Published:** 2026-05-23 · **URL:** https://www.cbsnews.com/losangeles/news/garden-grove-gkn-aerospace-chemical-leak-investigation-orange-county-district-attorney-whistleblowers/

## DA Investigation

Orange County District Attorney Todd Spitzer launched a criminal probe into GKN Aerospace on Saturday, May 23. His office began investigating Friday, deploying drones to survey the area and preserving evidence for potential litigation.

Spitzer stated: **"We are not getting satisfactory answers."**

He criticized the facility's absence of redundant safety systems: **"How in the world could this entity…not have any redundancy or backup systems?"**

## Whistleblower Outreach

Spitzer specifically appealed to GKN Aerospace employees: **"If you want to come forward and you want to be interviewed and you want to tell us what you know, now is the time."** He indicated preferential treatment would be extended to early whistleblowers over those who came forward later.

Anonymous Tip Hotline: **714-347-8714** (also accessible via the DA's online submission form).

## Temperature Readings

- Normal/optimal storage temperature: approximately **50°F**
- Saturday interior reading: hovering around **90°F**
- Rising rate: approximately **1°F per hour** despite external cooling efforts

## Evacuation

- **50,000 people** evacuated from homes around the GKN Aerospace facility at 12000 Western Avenue, Garden Grove
