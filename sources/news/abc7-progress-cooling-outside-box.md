# Fire official says crews making progress cooling tank

**Outlet:** ABC7 Los Angeles (KABC) · **Published:** May 23, 2026, 4:04 AM GMT (≈ 9:04 PM PDT May 22) · **URL:** https://abc7.com/live-updates/garden-grove-chemical-tank-emergency-leaking-toxic-chemicals-orange-county-will-spill-explode-officials-say/19152918/entry/19154034/

Live-blog entry reporting overnight May 22–23 cooling progress. OCFA incident commander Craig Covey reported exterior shell reading of approximately 61 °F and described a new set of mitigation strategies developed by responders brought in from around the country.

## Temperature reading

| Time | Value | Method |
|------|-------|--------|
| ~9 PM PDT May 22 (4:04 AM GMT May 23) | ~61 °F | Exterior / shell (cooling water spray) |
| Target | 50 °F | OCFA safe-neutralization threshold |

Note: interior gauge at approximately the same time was 90 °F (see entry/19157063 and nbc-la-temperature-increasing.md), confirming the exterior shell had been cooled far below interior bulk temperature.

## OCFA quotes (Craig Covey)

"The focus that we have been telling our team is to think outside the box."

"In the last four to five hours, they've come up with some tremendous ideas."

"Those efforts are succeeding, and it's giving us opportunity to reconsider engaging in close proximity to implement some of the concepts that were outside the box."

"It is not OK with me just to sit back and watch this thing blow up or fail. That is not acceptable to me."

## Key context

- Cooling efforts underway overnight May 22–23 via unmanned firehoses
- Exterior shell had been reduced to ~61 °F; interior remained at 90 °F and rising
- Experts brought in from across the country to develop novel mitigation strategies
- No injuries reported; evacuation zone maintained
