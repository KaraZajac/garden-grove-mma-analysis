# Orange County DA seeks tips regarding cause of chemical tank crisis

**Outlet:** ABC7 Los Angeles (KABC) · **Entry published:** May 24, 2026, 4:49 AM GMT · **URL:** https://abc7.com/live-updates/garden-grove-chemical-tank-emergency-leaking-toxic-chemicals-orange-county-will-spill-explode-officials-say/19152918/entry/19159693/

Live-blog entry announcing the Orange County District Attorney's tip channels for the criminal investigation into the GKN Aerospace MMA tank crisis.

## DA tip channels

- **Anonymous tip hotline:** (714) 347-8714
- **Online form:** www.ocdistrictattorney.gov
- **Email:** tipster@ocdistrictattorney.gov

DA seeks information about "circumstances of what led up to the incident, including but not limited to specific details of the release, information about the industrial operations of the facility, the quality and frequency of maintenance of the tanks and systems at issue."

## Evacuation status (as of entry)

Approximately 50,000 Orange County residents under mandatory evacuation orders across Garden Grove, West Anaheim, Cypress, Buena Park, and Stanton.

## Temperature status (as of entry)

Interior tank temperature "has been rising about one degree per hour since Thursday morning." No new measured reading cited.
