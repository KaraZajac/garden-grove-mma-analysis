# Crews to conduct all-night mission to confirm if explosion threat is eliminated

**Outlet:** ABC7 LA live-blog entry 19165547 · **Reported:** Sun May 24, 2026 (page last updated ~10:11 PM PDT / 5:11 AM GMT Mon) · **URL:** https://abc7.com/live-updates/garden-grove-chemical-tank-emergency-leaking-toxic-chemicals-orange-county-will-spill-explode-officials-say/19152918/entry/19165547/

OCFA confirmed that an all-night Sun→Mon recon/instrumentation mission would attempt to determine whether the crack discovered Sat night is actually venting pressure — and therefore whether the BLEVE scenario can be ruled out. A Monday-morning briefing was promised but not held by time of fetch.

## Key quote — Interim Chief TJ McGovern

> "The BLEVE threat is the worst-case catastrophic event that we have been talking about. We are not there yet; we need to run this operation tonight."

This explicitly contradicts the "step in the right direction" framing from Sunday morning: McGovern is now careful to say the BLEVE risk is **not** off the table pending overnight data.

## Operational facts (new since Sun afternoon)

- Internal temp still pegged >100 °F on the only working gauge; no new reading published.
- One **confirmed** crack — peeling exterior weather-stripping is *not* an additional crack (clarification walks back early "multiple cracks" wording).
- Cooling = high-flow water on the exterior; drones still monitoring thermal signature.
- Crews assessing whether the crack is **releasing pressure** or **was caused by vapor buildup** (i.e., a precursor to rupture rather than relief).
- Evacuation zone unchanged: ~9 sq mi, 50,000+ residents, Garden Grove PD reports a **15% refusal rate** on the order.
- Disneyland (5 mi) reports no operational impact.

## Federal/state escalation

- Newsom requested a federal emergency declaration from President Trump (submitted Sat night).
- 785+ state personnel deployed (see separate CalOES source).
